# Founding creator invite

Send from your own email, one person at a time. Change the first line for each person.

**Subject:** Would you show other people how you use AI?

Hi [first name],

I've seen how you use AI for [the thing they do: e.g. client reports / lesson planning / chasing invoices], and I think a lot of people would love to learn it from you.

I'm building fishpond, a free library of step-by-step AI guides for real tasks at work and at home. There are 507 guides in it so far, and I'm now inviting a small group of founding creators to add their own.

It's simple:

1. Screen-record yourself doing the task with AI and talk it through. Ten minutes is plenty.
2. We turn it into a guide with clear steps and ready-to-copy prompts. You check it before it goes live.
3. It goes into the library with your name on it.

Founding creators get their name on the first guides, credits when people use them, and a share of members' fees once paid membership launches.

Here's how it works and where to sign up: thefishpond.online/creators

Would you be up for recording one? Happy to jump on a 10-minute call if that's easier.

Thanks,
