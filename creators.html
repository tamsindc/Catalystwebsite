How it works

Step 1

Record it

Screen-record yourself doing the task, start to finish, and talk it through as you go. Ten minutes is plenty. No writing needed.

Step 2

We shape it

We turn your recording into a fishpond guide with clear steps, ready-to-copy prompts and a set-up kit. You check it before it goes live.

Step 3

It goes live with your name

Your guide joins the free library, credited to you by name. As profiles open, it will link to yours.

Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can share your screen on a video call, you can record a workflow. We do the writing.

Who owns my workflow?

You do. You give fishpond permission to publish and adapt it as a guide. The full creator terms will set this out in plain English before anything goes live.

What about private information in my recording?

Use made-up or blurred examples if you can. We check every recording and remove names, emails and figures before anything is published.

Can I record work tasks?

Yes, as long as you are allowed to share how you do them. Don’t show client data or anything your employer treats as confidential.

When will I get paid?

Cash payouts begin when paid Pro membership launches. Until then, founding creators build up credits and get their name on the first guides in the library.
