Most AI consultancies start with technology and hope people catch up. We do the opposite.

The Catalyst Method puts your people at the centre of every decision and every success metric. Because we've learned what others ignore: the best AI strategy in the world fails without human buy-in.

Every transformation starts with your people, not your systems.

We conduct comprehensive assessments that go beyond technology gaps. We listen to your team's concerns, understand their workflows, and identify where AI can genuinely help, not just where it could theoretically fit.

Our strategic roadmaps aren't generic tech documents. They're human-centered blueprints that respect your culture, match your team's readiness, and deliver real value at every stage.

What you get:
• Honest conversations about what AI can (and can't) do for your specific situation
• Assessments that evaluate both technical capability and human readiness
• Clear next steps that match your team's pace, not a vendor's timeline
• Roadmaps built around your people's needs, not the latest tech trends

Because the best technology strategy is one your team believes in.

2. Capability Building and Culture Change
Technology without adoption is expensive failure. Trust without training is false hope.

We transform your entire organization into confident AI collaborators who understand how to keep humans in the loop. Our immersive consultancy work doesn't just teach tools; we build genuine fluency and sustainable culture change.

Our approach:

Develop internal advocates who support colleagues, share wins, and drive adoption from the inside out.

We also have access to a wide external network of people that can do the things that we don't! No man is an island, especially in the world of AI. Ready to develop a product? Need someone to help you build? Or data cleanse? Or test? Catalyst has you covered!

Continuous Learning & Support
AI evolves quickly. Your team should too. Ongoing resources, refresher sessions, and a community that grows together.

We believe: AI should handle the boring stuff so humans can do the brilliant stuff.

3. Governance & Policy Development
Build the policies your organization needs to use AI responsibly

AI without clear policies is a liability waiting to happen. We help you develop practical governance frameworks that enable innovation while protecting your people, customers, and reputation.

What we create with you:

AI Use Policies and Guidelines
Comprehensive, plain-language policies that define when, where, and how AI can be used in your organization. Your team will actually understand and follow these, because we write for humans, not legal departments.

Human-in-the-Loop Protocols
Decision-making frameworks that specify which tasks AI can handle autonomously and which require human judgment. Clear escalation procedures for edge cases and ethical concerns.

Acceptable Use Policies
Guidelines for employees on appropriate AI tool usage, from ChatGPT to custom systems. What's allowed, what's prohibited, and why it matters.

Vendor Evaluation Frameworks
Criteria for assessing AI vendors and solutions: transparency requirements, human oversight capabilities, security standards, and exit strategies.

Training & Change Management Plans
Policies aren't worth the paper they're written on if nobody knows about them. We help you communicate and reinforce these standards across your organization.

Our approach:
We don't hand you a generic policy template and walk away. We facilitate workshops with your leadership and key stakeholders to develop policies that reflect your values, your risk tolerance, and your operational reality.

Every policy includes:
• Clear rationale (why this matters)
• Practical examples (what this looks like in practice)
• Implementation guidance (how to do this)
• Review schedules (policies should evolve as AI evolves)

Your people should always understand—and have a voice in—how AI is governed in your organization.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does your "pick and mix" approach work?
We start by understanding what you want to achieve and where your team is today. Maybe you're curious about AI but not ready for a major project; perfect. We'll recommend a small first step, like a half-day workshop or readiness assessment.

You can add more services later as you gain confidence and clarity. This way, you control the pace, the cost, and the commitment. No pressure, no long-term contracts until you're ready.

How do I know if my business is ready for AI?
Great question. We offer a simple readiness assessment—basically a structured conversation and brief survey to understand:
• What business problems you're trying to solve
• How your team feels about new technology
• What capabilities you already have
• Where AI might actually help (versus where it's just hype)

You'll receive a clear, honest summary of where to start, what benefits to expect, and what challenges to prepare for. Sometimes the answer is "not yet"—and we'll tell you that.

Will AI replace our workforce?
Not the way we implement it. Ever.

Our philosophy is simple and non-negotiable: AI augments humans, never replaces them.

We design systems where AI handles repetitive, time-consuming tasks so your people can focus on:
• Creative problem-solving
• Strategic thinking
• Relationship building
• Complex decision-making
• Work that requires empathy, judgment, and human insight

In every engagement, we've seen AI make jobs more interesting and create new opportunities—not eliminate positions. The goal is to make your team more effective, not smaller.

Can you help with AI strategy and policy for my organization?
Absolutely. We translate complex technical concepts and regulatory requirements into clear, actionable guidelines your entire organization can understand and follow.

We help boards and executives establish governance frameworks, ethical principles, and practical policies for responsible AI use—all written in plain English, not confusing legalese.

Our expertise spans the region, so we know how to meet Cayman's regulatory standards while following international best practices.

How is Catalyst different from other AI consultancies?
Most consultancies focus on deploying technology. We focus on transforming organizations.

While others sell tools and platforms, we build human capabilities. Our approach ensures sustainable adoption and continuous value creation long after our engagement ends—because your team knows how to use AI effectively and responsibly.

We also refuse to implement AI without proper human oversight. If a proposed solution doesn't keep humans in meaningful control, we won't recommend it. Period.

What size companies do you work with?
We work with organizations from small local businesses to large enterprises. Whether you have 10 employees or 1,000, our methodologies adapt to your scale and complexity.

What matters isn't your size—it's your commitment to doing AI the right way. If you're willing to invest in your people alongside your technology, we can help you succeed.

What happens after the engagement ends?
Our goal is your independence, not dependence.

We ensure you're self-sufficient through comprehensive knowledge transfer and capability building. You'll have the skills, documentation, and confidence to operate effectively on your own.

Many clients engage us for periodic strategic reviews or new initiatives, but day-to-day operations remain fully in your control. Optional ongoing support is available but never required.

How do I get started?
Simple. Click "Let's Talk Strategy" to schedule a free, no-obligation consultation.

We'll have an honest conversation about:
• Your current situation and goals
• How AI might (or might not) help
• What a practical next step could look like
• What working together would actually involve

You can start as small as a one-hour consultation. We'll answer all your questions, and you'll leave with clarity—whether you work with us or not.

No sales pressure. Just strategic insight from people who genuinely care about getting it right.
