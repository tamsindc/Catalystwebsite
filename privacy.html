Privacy notice

Short version: we only collect what you type into a sign-up form, we use it only to contact you about what you signed up for, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

fishpond (thefishpond.online) is run by Tamsin Deasey-Weinstein. For anything about your data, email [contact email].

What we collect

  • When you sign up: your email address, and anything else you choose to give, such as your name, organisation, job, a description of a task or a link to a recording.
  • Which list you joined (for example the waitlist or founding creators) and the page you signed up on.

The guides themselves work without an account. Prompts you fill in on a guide stay in your browser and are not sent to us. Saved guides are kept in your own browser. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when the page loads. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

Only to contact you about the thing you signed up for, such as launch news, becoming a creator, a Rung cohort or a conversation about hiring. We rely on your consent. We never sell your details or use them for advertising.

Where it is kept

Sign-ups are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and the website is hosted by Vercel. Both may process data outside the UK under standard data protection safeguards.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or for up to two years after we last contact you, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
