Turn school and club emails into calendar events

Dates buried in school newsletters and club emails become calendar events with reminders, so nothing like non-uniform day sneaks up on you.

Personal AssistantIntermediate20 minGmailGoogle CalendarAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Whenever a school or club email arrives

Step by step

  1. Label the emails

    In Gmail's settings, under Filters, create a filter for emails from the school and clubs and give them a label such as Kids. In Outlook, a rule can move them to a folder.

  2. Pull out the dates

    When a newsletter arrives, copy its text into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyRead this school or club email and list every date, deadline or event. For each one, give: date, time if mentioned, title (short, for a calendar), who it's for, what we need to do or bring, and whether it needs a reply, form or payment by a deadline. Return a table sorted by date. Then list any action I need to take this week. [paste email]

  3. Add them to the calendar

    Add each event to your family calendar. Gmail sometimes offers to add events it spots in emails too, so check for that first.

  4. Set reminders for deadlines

    For forms, payments and things to bring, set the reminder for the evening before so there's time to prepare.

What you get

Fri 22 Nov | All day | Non-uniform day | Both children | Bring £1 each Wed 27 Nov | 3:30pm | Year 3 class assembly | Ellie | Parents welcome By Mon 25 Nov | Trip consent and £8 via school app | Max | Action needed

Tips

  • Avoid pasting children's full names. The AI doesn't need them.
  • If your plan includes Gemini in Gmail, you can ask it to find dates in your Kids label directly.
