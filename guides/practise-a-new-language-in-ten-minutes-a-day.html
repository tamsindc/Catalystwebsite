Practise a new language in ten minutes a day

A daily conversation partner that chats with you at your level, gently corrects mistakes and keeps a list of words to review.

LearningBeginner5 minAI assistantNotes app
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every day, at the same time

Step by step

  1. Pick a time and topic

    Choose a regular slot, such as your commute or after dinner, and a topic you'd actually talk about, like your day, food or travel plans.

  2. Start the conversation

    Open your AI assistant, ideally in voice mode for speaking practice, and paste this prompt.

    Prompt to copyBe my [language] conversation partner. My level is [beginner / A2 / intermediate]. Today's topic: [topic]. Speak only in [language] using vocabulary at my level, with short sentences. Ask me one question at a time. After each of my replies, first answer naturally, then add a short 'Correction' line showing any mistake and the correct version, with a one-line explanation in English. After 10 exchanges, give me a list of 8 new or tricky words and phrases with English meanings.

  3. Save the word list

    Copy the word list at the end into a note called Language words so you can review it later.

  4. Repeat tomorrow with a twist

    Next day, start by pasting yesterday's word list and ask the AI to use those words again in a new conversation.

What you get

AI: ¿Qué hiciste el fin de semana? You: Yo fui al mercado y compré frutas. AI: ¡Qué bien! ¿Qué frutas compraste? Correction: none, nice use of the past tense. Words today: el mercado (market), madrugar (to get up early)...

Tips

  • Speaking out loud in voice mode builds confidence faster than typing.
  • AI is a great practice partner but can sound formal. Mix in podcasts or shows for how people really speak.
