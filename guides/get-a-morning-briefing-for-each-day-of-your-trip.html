Get a morning briefing for each day of your trip

Wake up to a short daily message with today's plan, bookings, weather to check and one local tip.

TravelAdvanced30 minAI assistantGoogle DocsGoogle Calendar
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every morning while you're away

Step by step

  1. Finish your trip document

    Make sure your itinerary and booking summary are in one Google Docs or Word file, with times in local time.

  2. Put key times in your calendar

    Add bookings like tours, trains and restaurant tables to Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar with reminders, so you get an alert whatever happens.

  3. Set up a scheduled daily task

    If your AI assistant offers scheduled tasks (ChatGPT does on some plans), create a daily task for each morning of the trip and paste in your itinerary with this prompt. Otherwise, set a daily phone reminder and paste the prompt yourself.

    Prompt to copyEvery morning at [time, local] from [start date] to [end date], send me a short briefing for that day of my trip to [destination], using this itinerary: [paste itinerary]. Include: today's plan in order with times, any bookings and their reference, how to get between places, what to pack in my day bag, a reminder to check today's weather, and one local tip or phrase. Keep it under 150 words.

  4. Adjust on the go

    If plans change, reply to the task or edit the itinerary in it so tomorrow's briefing is right.

What you get

Wed, Day 3: 09:15 train to Sintra (Rossio station, buy tickets at the machine). 10:30 Pena Palace, timed entry, ref PP4471. Lunch in the village. Day bag: water, layers, comfy shoes - lots of hills. Check the weather: mist is common. Phrase: 'A conta, por favor' - the bill, please.

Tips

  • Keep a copy of your bookings offline too, in case you lose signal or the briefing doesn't arrive.
