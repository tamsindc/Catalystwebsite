Work out what's wrong with a struggling plant

Photograph a sick-looking houseplant or garden plant and get likely causes and a simple care plan to try.

Food & HomeBeginner3 minAI assistantPhone camera
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

When leaves go yellow, brown or spotty

Step by step

  1. Take close and wide photos

    Photograph the whole plant, a close-up of the damaged leaves (top and underside) and the soil.

  2. Ask for likely causes

    Attach the photos to your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyPlease help me work out what's wrong with this plant. It's a [plant name if known] kept [indoors/outdoors] in [light conditions]. I water it [how often] and it's been like this for [time]. Recent changes: [repotting, moved, cold snap]. Give me the 3 most likely causes ranked by likelihood, what to look for to tell them apart, and a simple 2-week care plan for the most likely one. If you can't identify the plant, say so.

  3. Set a check-in reminder

    Add a reminder for two weeks' time to see if it's improving, and take a new photo to compare.

What you get

Most likely: overwatering (yellow lower leaves, damp soil). Check: push a finger 3cm into the soil - if wet, wait. Plan: water only when top 3cm is dry, empty the saucer, move away from the radiator. Less likely: spider mites - look for fine webbing under leaves.

Tips

  • AI can misidentify plants, so if it might be toxic to pets or children, check with a garden centre or a reliable plant database.
