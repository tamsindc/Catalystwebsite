Manage holiday requests without clashes or confusion

Staff request leave through a form, you see clashes highlighted in a sheet, get an alert, and approved days go straight onto a shared team calendar.

HR & HiringAdvanced50 minGoogle FormsGoogle SheetsGoogle CalendarAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every time someone asks for time off

Step by step

  1. Create the request form

    Make a Google Form with: name (dropdown of staff), first day off, last day off, type of leave (holiday, unpaid, other) and an optional note. Turn on email notifications for new responses.

  2. Set up the sheet with AI help

    Link the form to a Google Sheet, then ask your AI assistant for the formulas you need.

    Prompt to copyMy Google Sheet of holiday requests has these columns: A Timestamp, B Name, C First day, D Last day, E Leave type, F Note. Give me beginner-friendly, step-by-step instructions to: 1) add column G 'Working days' that counts weekdays between C and D (using NETWORKDAYS), 2) add column H 'Status' with a dropdown of Pending, Approved, Declined, 3) highlight a row orange if its dates overlap with another Approved request, using a custom formula in conditional formatting, 4) make a summary tab showing each person's approved days so far this year against an allowance of [number] days. Give the exact formulas and say where to paste them.

  3. Make a shared team calendar

    Create a separate Google Calendar called Team holidays and share it with staff as view-only.

  4. Approve and add to the calendar

    When an alert arrives, check for orange clashes, set the Status, reply to the person, and add approved leave as an all-day event on the team calendar.

  5. Check balances monthly

    Look at the summary tab once a month and remind anyone who has lots of leave left.

What you get

New request: Marta | 12 to 16 Aug | Holiday | 5 working days | Pending (orange: overlaps with Dan, Approved 14 to 21 Aug). Summary: Marta 9 of 28 days used; Dan 17 of 28. Team holidays calendar: 'Dan - holiday' 14 to 21 Aug.

Tips

  • Keep 'other' leave notes brief and private; sickness and family reasons are sensitive personal data.
  • Check that formulas work with a few test requests before you share the form with the team.
