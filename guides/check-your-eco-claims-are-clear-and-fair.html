Check your eco claims are clear and fair

Your green and sustainability wording reviewed for vague or risky phrases, with clearer alternatives and a list of evidence to keep on file.

Marketing managerBeginner10 minAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Before publishing packaging, ads or web copy with environmental claims

Step by step

  1. Collect the wording

    Copy every environmental claim from your draft packaging, web page or ad into one place, along with the facts you have to back each one up.

  2. Ask for a careful review

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyReview these environmental claims for [product] as a careful marketing compliance reviewer. For each claim give: 1) whether it is specific or vague, 2) what a sceptical customer might ask, 3) what evidence we would need to support it, 4) a clearer, more precise rewrite. Flag any words like 'eco-friendly', 'green' or 'sustainable' used without detail. Present it as a table. Do not invent facts about the product; where the evidence is missing, say so. Claims and the evidence we have: [paste claims and facts]

  3. Gather the proof

    Use the evidence column as a checklist and save the supporting documents in one shared folder.

  4. Get a final sign-off

    Run the final wording past your legal or compliance contact, as rules on green claims differ by country.

What you get

Claim: 'Eco-friendly packaging.' Verdict: vague. Customer might ask: 'Friendly how?' Evidence needed: material breakdown, recyclability certificate. Rewrite: 'Box made from 80% recycled card. Recycle with household paper.'

Tips

  • This is a first check, not legal advice. Always confirm claims with a qualified person before launch.
