Look back on your month and plan the next

Answer five quick questions and get a short monthly review: what went well, what didn't and three focus points for next month.

Planning & ProductivityBeginner15 minAI assistantNotes appGoogle Calendar
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Last Sunday of every month

Step by step

  1. Scroll back through your month

    Look at your calendar, photos and to-do lists from the last four weeks to jog your memory.

  2. Talk it through with AI

    Paste this prompt and answer the questions it asks, one at a time.

    Prompt to copyRun a monthly review with me. Ask me these questions one at a time and wait for each answer: 1) What went well this month? 2) What didn't go to plan, and why? 3) What drained my energy? 4) What gave me energy? 5) What do I want more of next month? Then write a summary with: 3 wins, 2 lessons, and 3 specific focus points for [next month], each with one small action I can put in my calendar. Keep it encouraging and honest.

  3. Schedule the focus points

    Add each small action to your calendar for next month, and save the summary in a 'Monthly reviews' note.

  4. Make it recurring

    Create a monthly repeating calendar event so the review happens without you having to remember.

What you get

Wins: Finished the website refresh; ran 3 times a week; booked summer holiday. Lessons: Saying yes to Saturday work wore me out. Focus for December: Protect weekends (block Saturdays); finish online course (2 x 30 min a week); call Gran fortnightly.

Tips

  • Read last month's summary before you start, so you can see whether last month's focus points happened.
