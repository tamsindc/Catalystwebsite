Keep your pet's care on track with shared reminders

Set up reminders for flea, worming, vaccinations and vet visits that everyone at home can see.

Food & HomeIntermediate20 minAI assistantGoogle CalendarNotes app
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Set up once, update after each vet visit

Step by step

  1. Gather your pet's records

    Find your pet's vaccination card, last flea and worming dates and the products the vet recommended.

  2. Turn it into a care schedule

    Paste the details into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHelp me organise my pet's routine care. Pet: [species, breed, age, weight]. Last vaccination: [date and type]. Flea treatment: [product and date, and how often the vet said]. Worming: [product, date, frequency]. Other: [nail trims, grooming, dental checks, insurance renewal]. Create a 12-month schedule table with date, task and notes, using only the frequencies I've given. Don't recommend products or doses. Add a short list of questions to ask the vet at the next check-up.

  3. Add shared reminders

    Create recurring events in a shared Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar so everyone at home sees when the next treatment is due.

  4. Keep a care note

    Save a note with the vet's number, insurance policy number, microchip number and any allergies, so it's handy in an emergency.

What you get

Bramble (cat, 4): Flea - 1st of each month. Worming - every 3 months: 10 Jan, 10 Apr, 10 Jul, 10 Oct. Booster - 18 May (book 2 weeks before). Insurance renews 1 Sept. Ask vet: is her weight OK? Should we brush her teeth?

Tips

  • Always follow your vet's advice on products and doses. The AI is only for organising dates.
