Turn a podcast episode into show notes and clips

From one episode transcript, get show notes, chapter times, quotable moments and a list of short clips worth cutting for social.

Video, Image & VoiceBeginner15 minAI assistantYouTube
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

After recording each episode

Step by step

  1. Get a transcript

    Use the transcript from your recording or hosting tool, or upload the episode to YouTube as unlisted and copy the automatic captions from YouTube Studio.

  2. Ask for show notes and clip picks

    Paste the transcript into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere's the transcript of my podcast episode: [paste]. Show: [name] for [audience]. Guest: [name, if any]. Give me: 1) 3 episode title options; 2) show notes: a 100-word summary and 5 bullet key takeaways; 3) chapter markers with timestamps; 4) 5 clips of 30-60 seconds for social media, each with start and end time, why it works on its own, and a hook line for on-screen text; 5) 3 short quotes, word for word. Only use what's in the transcript.

  3. Cut the clips

    Use the timestamps to trim clips in your phone editor, Canva or YouTube Shorts' tools, and add the hook as on-screen text.

What you get

Title: Why Small Charities Burn Out (and How to Stop It). Clip 2: 14:32-15:20, guest explains 'the volunteer cliff' - works alone because it's a complete story. Hook: 'Every charity hits this at year three.'

Tips

  • Let your guest know you'll be sharing clips, and send them the links so they can share too.
