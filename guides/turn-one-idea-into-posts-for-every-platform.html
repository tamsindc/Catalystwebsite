Turn one idea into posts for every platform

Write one rough idea and get a LinkedIn post, an Instagram caption, a Facebook post and a short video hook, each in the right style.

Marketing & SocialBeginner10 minAI assistantNotes app
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Whenever you have something worth sharing

Step by step

  1. Jot down the idea in plain words

    In your notes app, write two or three sentences about the news, tip or story. Don't polish it, just get the point down.

  2. Ask your AI assistant to adapt it

    Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and paste this prompt with your idea.

    Prompt to copyI run [type of business] for [audience]. Here is a rough idea I want to share: [your idea]. Turn it into: 1) a LinkedIn post (120-180 words, a strong first line, short paragraphs, end with a question); 2) an Instagram caption (under 100 words, friendly, 5 relevant hashtags at the end); 3) a Facebook post (conversational, under 80 words, with a clear call to action: [what you want people to do]); 4) three opening lines for a 30-second video. Use British English, no hype words, and keep the facts exactly as I gave them.

  3. Make it sound like you

    Read each version aloud and change anything you wouldn't say. Ask the assistant to 'make it more casual' or 'shorter' if a version feels off.

  4. Post or schedule each one

    Copy each version into its app. Most platforms let you schedule a post for later if you'd rather spread them across the week.

What you get

LinkedIn: 'Most of our customers ask the same question in their first week...' Instagram: 'The one thing we wish every new customer knew...' +#smallbusiness #tips. Facebook: 'Quick tip from the team: ... Pop in on Saturday and we'll show you.' Video hooks: 'Stop doing this in week one.'

Tips

  • Save the prompt in your notes app with your business details filled in, so next time you only paste the idea.
  • Check any facts, prices or dates before posting; the assistant can quietly change them.
