Make flashcards from anything you're learning

A ready-to-import set of flashcards from your notes, a chapter or a vocab list, for apps like Anki or Quizlet.

LearningBeginner10 minAI assistantGoogle Sheets
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Whenever you finish a chapter or topic

Step by step

  1. Collect the material

    Copy the notes, chapter summary or word list you want to learn.

  2. Generate the cards

    Paste it into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyTurn the material below into 25 flashcards for [subject and level]. Each card should test one idea only. Front: a clear question or prompt (not just a single word unless it's vocabulary). Back: a short answer, under 25 words. Include a few cards that ask 'why' or 'what's the difference between' rather than just facts. Return the cards as two columns separated by a comma: Front,Back, with no header and no numbering, so I can import them into a flashcard app. [paste material]

  3. Check and tidy

    Read through the cards, delete any that are wrong or not useful, and fix anything that doesn't match your course.

  4. Import into your flashcard app

    Paste the list into a Google Sheet, export as CSV, and import it into a flashcard app such as Anki or Quizlet, which both support importing lists.

What you get

Tips

  • Fewer, better cards beat hundreds of rushed ones.
  • Ask it to avoid commas inside answers if your import keeps splitting cards in the wrong place.
