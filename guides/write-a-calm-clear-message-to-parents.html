Write a calm, clear message to parents

A kind, professional email or letter to a parent or carer about progress, behaviour or a concern, ready to personalise and send.

TeacherBeginner5 minAI assistantOutlook
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

When you need to contact a parent or carer about something tricky

Step by step

  1. Note the situation without names

    Write a short, general description, for example 'pupil has missed three homework deadlines but is doing well in class'. Leave out names and any personal details.

  2. Ask for a balanced draft

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyWrite a short email from a [subject] teacher to a parent or carer. Situation: [general description, no names]. Tone: warm, calm and professional, working together rather than blaming. Structure: 1) start with something positive about the pupil, 2) explain the concern with specific, factual examples, 3) say what I'm doing to help in school, 4) suggest one or two things that would help at home, 5) invite them to reply or meet, with [how to contact]. Under 180 words. Use [Pupil name] and [Parent name] as placeholders.

  3. Personalise it in your school email

    Copy the draft into your school email system, add the names and any details there, and check it follows your school's communication policy.

What you get

Dear [Parent name], [Pupil name] has been contributing brilliantly in our class discussions this term. I wanted to let you know that three recent homework tasks haven't been handed in. In school, I've offered a lunchtime homework club on Tuesdays. At home, a quick check of the planner each Sunday could really help...

Tips

  • Keep names and personal details out of the AI tool and add them only in your school email.
  • For safeguarding or serious concerns, follow your school's procedures rather than emailing.
