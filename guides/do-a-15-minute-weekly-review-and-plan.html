Do a 15-minute weekly review and plan

Every Friday you get a summary of what you got done, what's still open and a suggested plan for next week, built from your calendar and tasks.

Personal AssistantAdvanced40 minAI assistantGoogle CalendarNotion
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every Friday afternoon

Step by step

  1. Keep tasks in one place

    Use one task list, such as a Notion page, Google Tasks or your Reminders app, and mark things done as you go.

  2. Write your weekly review prompt

    Save this prompt in your notes.

    Prompt to copyRun my weekly review. Here's this week's calendar, my task list with done and not done items, and my goals for this month: [paste all three, or read them from my connected apps]. Give me: 1) what I got done this week in 5 bullets, 2) open tasks, sorted into carry over, delegate or drop, 3) next week's calendar with any busy days or clashes flagged, 4) a suggested plan of no more than 3 priorities for next week, with the best time slots to do them, and 5) one thing to celebrate. Keep it under 250 words and kind.

  3. Schedule it

    If your plan includes scheduled tasks in your AI assistant and it can read your calendar, schedule this for Fridays at 3pm. Otherwise add a recurring Friday calendar event and paste in your calendar and tasks yourself.

  4. Block time for priorities

    Add the three priorities to next week's calendar as time blocks so they actually happen.

  5. Adjust your goals monthly

    At the end of each month, update the goals in the prompt.

What you get

Done: booked boiler service, finished tax return, two gym sessions. Carry over: sort Gran's birthday present. Drop: re-paint shed (not this month). Next week: Wednesday is packed, avoid adding anything. Priorities: buy present (Mon lunch), plan half-term (Tue evening), call bank (Thu 9am). Celebrate: tax return done!

Tips

  • Keep priorities to three. More than that and none get done.
  • Only connect the apps you're comfortable with your AI assistant reading.
