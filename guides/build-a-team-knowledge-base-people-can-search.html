Build a team knowledge base people can search

Gather your team's how-tos and policies into Notion so anyone can find answers fast, written up clearly from your messy notes.

Documents & DataIntermediate60 minNotionAI assistantGoogle Docs
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Set up once, then add to it weekly

Step by step

  1. List the questions people keep asking

    Spend a week noting every 'how do I...' or 'where is...' question on your team. These become your first pages.

  2. Create a simple structure

    In Notion, make a page called 'Team handbook' with sub-pages for each area, such as Customers, Tools, Money and HR.

  3. Turn messy notes into clear pages

    Paste existing documents or emails into your AI assistant with this prompt, then copy the result into Notion.

    Prompt to copyTurn the notes below into a clear knowledge-base article titled '[title]'. Format: a one-line summary, 'When to use this', numbered steps, 'Who to ask', and 'Last checked: [date]'. Keep it short, use plain language, and keep every specific detail (names of tools, amounts, deadlines) exactly as written. List anything that seems out of date or contradictory at the end. [paste notes]

  4. Let people ask questions

    If your plan includes Notion AI, people can ask questions and get answers drawn from your pages. If not, Notion's search finds pages by keyword.

  5. Keep it fresh

    Give each page an owner and set a recurring reminder to check it every three months.

What you get

Article: How to process a refund. Summary: refunds under £50 can be approved by anyone on the team. Steps: 1) Find the order in Shopify. 2) Click Refund... Who to ask: Priya (over £50). Last checked: 9 Nov 2026. Possibly out of date: mentions the old returns address.

Tips

  • Keep passwords and personal staff details out of the knowledge base; say where they're kept instead.
