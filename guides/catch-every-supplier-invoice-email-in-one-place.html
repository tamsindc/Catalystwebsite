Catch every supplier invoice email in one place

Automatically label incoming invoice emails, then log them weekly in a sheet with supplier, amount and due date so nothing gets paid late.

Small Business AdminIntermediate25 minGmailGoogle SheetsAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Whenever a new invoice email arrives, plus a weekly log-up

Step by step

  1. Create an invoice filter

    In Gmail's settings, under Filters, create a filter for emails containing words like 'invoice' or 'statement' that have an attachment, and apply the label 'Invoices to pay'. In Outlook, create a rule that moves them to a folder.

  2. Set up a bills sheet

    Make a Google Sheet with columns: Received, Supplier, Invoice no., Amount, Due date, Paid?, Notes.

  3. Log them each week

    Once a week, open the label, upload the invoice PDFs to your AI assistant and paste this prompt.

    Prompt to copyExtract these details from each attached invoice into a table: Received date, Supplier, Invoice number, Amount (including VAT), Due date (DD/MM/YYYY), Payment details shown (yes/no). Copy everything exactly as printed. If a due date isn't shown, write 'not stated'. Do not include bank account numbers in your answer.

  4. Paste and sort

    Paste the rows into your sheet and sort by due date. Pay or schedule anything due in the next 7 days.

  5. Watch for fake invoices

    If an invoice comes from a new sender or a supplier's bank details have changed, phone the supplier on a number you already have before paying.

  • Check what your filter catches for the first couple of weeks and add or remove keywords as needed.
