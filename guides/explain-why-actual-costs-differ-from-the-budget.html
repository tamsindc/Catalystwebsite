Explain why actual costs differ from the budget

Turn a budget-versus-actual table into clear, short commentary managers understand, with questions to confirm before it goes out.

AccountantBeginner15 minAI assistantExcelWord
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Each month after the books close

Step by step

  1. Export budget versus actual

    Export a report from your accounting or budgeting tool showing budget, actual and variance for each line for the month and year to date.

  2. Add what you already know

    Jot down known reasons for big differences, such as a delayed project or a one-off repair. The AI writes better commentary when it has the real story.

  3. Draft the commentary

    Attach the report to your AI assistant and use this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere is the budget versus actual report for [department / company] for [month], plus my notes on known reasons. Write variance commentary for managers who aren't accountants. Include: 1) A three-sentence headline on overall performance. 2) A table of the lines that differ by more than [10]% or [£2,000]: Line | Budget | Actual | Variance | Likely reason | Timing or permanent? 3) Lines where I haven't given a reason, marked [ASK]. Use plain English, no jargon, and don't speculate beyond my notes. My notes: [paste]

  4. Confirm the reasons

    Check each reason with the budget holder, especially any marked [ASK]. Change anything that isn't right before you send it.

  5. Send it with the report

    Paste the final commentary into Word or your report pack next to the figures.

What you get

Headline: Spending was £14,200 under budget for October, mostly timing. Marketing £6,000 under (campaign moved to November, timing). Repairs £3,400 over (emergency roof repair, permanent). IT software £1,900 over [ASK].

Tips

  • Ask for a short version for a slide and a longer one for the finance pack from the same chat.
  • Don't paste client personal data, full account numbers or payroll details into a public AI tool unless your organisation allows it. Use your work-approved assistant if you have one.
