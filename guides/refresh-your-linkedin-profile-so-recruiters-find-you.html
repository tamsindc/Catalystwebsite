Refresh your LinkedIn profile so recruiters find you

A clearer LinkedIn headline, About section and experience bullets based on your real background and the roles you want next.

CareersBeginner20 minLinkedInAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Before a job search, then twice a year

Step by step

  1. Copy your current profile

    Copy your headline, About section and the text of your last two or three roles from LinkedIn.

  2. Ask for improvements

    Paste them into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHelp me improve my LinkedIn profile. I want to be found for roles like [target roles] in [sector or location]. Here's my current profile: [paste]. Using only my real experience, give me: 3 headline options under 220 characters that include words recruiters search for, an About section under 200 words in first person and a friendly tone, 3 to 4 bullet points for each of my last two roles focusing on results, and 10 skills to add that I've clearly shown. Don't invent achievements; ask me questions if a result could be made more specific.

  3. Add real numbers

    Answer the AI's questions with genuine figures, such as team size, customers served or time saved, to make your bullets stronger.

  4. Update and switch on Open to Work

    Paste the changes into LinkedIn. If you're job hunting, consider the Open to Work setting, which can be shown to recruiters only.

What you get

Headline: Customer service team leader | Training and coaching | Moving into Learning & Development. About: 'For six years I've helped new starters find their feet...' Bullet: 'Trained 30+ new advisors, with 90% passing probation first time.'

Tips

  • Keep your LinkedIn and CV consistent. Recruiters compare them.
  • Choose the recruiters-only Open to Work option if you don't want your current employer to see it.
