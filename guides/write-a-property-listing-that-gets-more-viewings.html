Write a property listing that gets more viewings

A clear, appealing property description, key features list and short social post from your photos and notes, written in minutes.

Estate agentBeginner10 minAI assistantPhone cameraNotes app
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

After every valuation or new instruction

Step by step

  1. Note the facts on the visit

    In your notes app, record room sizes, the best features, local selling points and anything the seller loves about the home. Snap the main rooms on your phone.

  2. Ask for a draft listing

    Open your AI assistant, attach a few photos and paste this prompt with your notes.

    Prompt to copyWrite a property listing for a [property type] for [sale / rent] at [asking price]. Ideal buyer: [e.g. first-time buyers, growing families, downsizers]. Using my notes and photos, give me: 1) A headline of max 10 words, 2) A 120-word description that leads with the best feature, 3) 8 bullet key features, 4) A room-by-room section using my measurements, 5) A 40-word social post. Warm, honest, no clichés like 'deceptively spacious' or 'must be seen'. Only state facts from my notes; if something is unclear, leave a [CHECK]. Notes: [paste notes]

  3. Check the facts

    Check every measurement, feature and claim against your notes and the seller. Listings must be accurate, so remove anything you can't back up.

  4. Publish

    Paste the text into your property portal and website listings, and use the social post for your agency's pages.

What you get

Headline: Sunny three-bed with a south-facing garden Description: Morning light fills the kitchen-diner of this three-bedroom semi, set on a quiet street a short walk from the station... Key features: south-facing garden, new boiler 2023, driveway for two cars...

Tips

  • Don't describe who 'should' live there in ways that exclude people, such as 'perfect for young professionals only'. Describe the home, not the buyer.
  • Keep the seller's details and address number out of the prompt if you don't need them.
