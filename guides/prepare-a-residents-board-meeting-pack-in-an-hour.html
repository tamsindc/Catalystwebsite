Prepare a residents' board meeting pack in an hour

A clear agenda, short summary papers and decisions needed for a residents' association or board meeting, from your notes and reports.

Property managerBeginner30 minAI assistantWordGoogle Docs
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

A week before each board or residents' meeting

Step by step

  1. Gather the inputs

    Collect the last minutes, open actions, budget figures, repair updates and any disputes or issues to raise. Leave out residents' names where they're not needed.

  2. Ask for a draft pack

    Paste the material with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyI manage a [building / estate] and need a board meeting pack. From my notes, create: 1) A timed agenda (max 90 minutes), 2) For each item a half-page paper: background, current position, options, and the decision needed from the board, 3) An updated action log from the last minutes, showing done, in progress or overdue, 4) A one-paragraph cover note for board members. Use only my figures. Be neutral on disputes between residents. Notes: [paste]

  3. Check figures and sensitive items

    Check every number against your accounts. For legal disputes or environmental issues, ask your legal or specialist adviser to review the paper before it's circulated.

  4. Send it out

    Format the pack in your template and send it to board members with enough time to read it.

What you get

Item 3: Roof repairs (15 min) Background: survey found worn flashing on the north side. Options: patch repair now, or full replacement in the next financial year. Decision needed: approve patch repair quote within the reserve fund limit.

Tips

  • Put 'decision needed' in bold on every paper. Meetings go faster when everyone knows what they're deciding.
  • Minutes and action logs from this pack make next month's pack quicker.
