Choose the right neighbourhood to stay in

When to use it

Before booking accommodation

Step by step

  1. Decide what matters

    Note your budget per night, who's travelling and your priorities, like quiet nights, nightlife, walking to sights or good transport.

  2. Ask for a comparison

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyI'm visiting [city] for [number] nights with [who]. Budget: about [amount] per night. Priorities: [e.g. quiet at night, walkable, near the station, family-friendly]. Compare the [4] best neighbourhoods to stay in using a table with: general feel, best for, getting to the main sights, noise, typical price level, and one downside. Then recommend one, explain why, and list what to search for in reviews to confirm it.

  3. Check on the map

    Drop the areas into Google Maps to see the walking distance to your top sights, and read recent reviews of places you're considering.

What you get

Alfama: charming, hilly, can be noisy on weekends. Baixa: central, flat, great transport, busy. Principe Real: quiet, leafy, pricier. Recommended: Baixa for your first visit with a buggy. Search reviews for: 'lift', 'street noise', 'stairs'.

Tips

  • Area reputations change, so check recent reviews and official travel advice rather than relying on AI alone.
