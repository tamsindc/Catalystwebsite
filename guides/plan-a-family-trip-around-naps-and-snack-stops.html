When to use it

When planning a trip with young children

Step by step

  1. Write down your children's rhythms

    Note each child's age, nap times, bedtime, meal times and what keeps them happy.

  2. Ask for a family-friendly plan

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyPlan a [number]-day trip to [destination] for [adults] adults and children aged [ages]. Naps: [times]. Bedtime: [time]. They love [interests] and struggle with [e.g. long queues, car journeys over an hour]. For each day: one main activity in the morning, a calm option during naps, playgrounds or open spaces nearby, places with baby changing and family-friendly food, and a backup if it rains or someone's had enough. Include travel day tips for the journey there.

  3. Check facilities and prices

    Look up each place's official website for family tickets, buggy access, opening times and whether booking is needed.

What you get

Day 1: 9:30 aquarium (buggy friendly, under-3s free - check site), 11:45 lunch at the cafe on site. 12:30-2pm nap in the buggy on a park walk. 3pm playground by the harbour. Backup: soft play near the hotel. Journey: leave after breakfast, stop every 90 mins.

Tips

  • Build in one completely unplanned half-day: children rarely keep to a schedule on holiday.
