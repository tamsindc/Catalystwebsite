Send a thoughtful thank-you after every interview

A short, personal follow-up email drafted the same day as your interview, mentioning something specific you discussed.

CareersIntermediate10 minGmailGoogle CalendarAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Within a day of each interview

Step by step

  1. Make quick notes straight after

    Right after the interview, record a voice note or jot down who you met, what you talked about, and anything you wish you'd said.

  2. Set a same-day reminder

    When you add an interview to your calendar, add a second short event an hour after it ends called 'Send thank-you email'.

  3. Draft the email

    Paste your notes into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyWrite a short thank-you email after my interview for [role] at [organisation] with [interviewer names]. Notes from the interview: [paste notes]. Keep it under 120 words, warm and professional. Mention one specific thing we discussed, briefly add [one point I forgot to make, if any], and restate my interest. No grovelling or over-the-top language. Give a subject line too.

  4. Send from your email

    Paste the draft into a new email in Gmail or Outlook, check names are spelled correctly, and send it.

What you get

Subject: Thank you - Volunteer Coordinator interview Hi Priya and Tom, thank you for your time today. I enjoyed hearing about the plans for the new food bank hub, and it made me even more keen to help build the volunteer team. One thing I didn't mention: I'm trained in safeguarding...

Tips

  • Use a voice note straight after the interview while details are fresh.
  • If you didn't get the interviewer's email, send it to the recruiter or HR contact and ask them to pass it on.
