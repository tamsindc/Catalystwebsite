Write YouTube titles, descriptions and chapters in minutes

From your video's transcript, get title options, a full description, timestamped chapters and tags ready to paste into YouTube Studio.

Video, Image & VoiceBeginner10 minYouTubeAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every time you upload a video

Step by step

  1. Get the transcript

    Upload your video to YouTube Studio as private or unlisted and wait for automatic captions. In the Subtitles section you can open and copy or download the captions with timings.

  2. Ask for the details

    Paste the transcript into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere is the timed transcript of my YouTube video: [paste]. Channel: [channel topic] for [audience]. Give me: 1) 5 title options under 60 characters, main search phrase near the start, honest (no clickbait); 2) a description: two opening lines that make sense in search results, then a 100-word summary, then [links/CTA]; 3) chapters starting at 0:00, at least 3, each with a short clear name, using the real times from the transcript; 4) 10 tags; 5) 3 ideas for thumbnail text (max 4 words).

  3. Paste into YouTube Studio

    Add the title, description (chapters go at the bottom, each on its own line starting with the timestamp) and tags, then publish or schedule the video.

What you get

Title: How to Re-Grout a Shower in One Afternoon. Chapters: 0:00 What you'll need, 1:12 Removing old grout, 4:30 Mixing new grout, 7:05 Applying and cleaning, 10:40 Sealing. Thumbnail text: 'Like new in 4 hours'.

Tips

  • Double-check chapter times by clicking each one after publishing; auto captions can drift slightly.
