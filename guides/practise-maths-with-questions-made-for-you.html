When to use it

When you need extra practice

Step by step

  1. Pick the topic

    Choose one topic, like percentages, fractions or solving equations, and note the level you're working at.

  2. Start a practice session

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyGive me practice questions on [maths topic] at [level, for example Year 8, GCSE Foundation, adult brush-up]. Start easy. Give one question at a time and wait for my answer. If I'm right, make the next one slightly harder. If I'm wrong, show a clear worked solution step by step, then give me a similar question to try again. Include some word problems from everyday life like shopping or cooking. After 10 questions, tell me what I'm good at and what to practise next.

  3. Work it out on paper

    Do the working on paper rather than in your head, then type your final answer.

  4. Double-check calculations

    If an answer seems wrong, check it with a calculator. AI can occasionally make arithmetic slips.

What you get

Q4: A jacket costs £60 and is reduced by 15%. What's the sale price? You: £51. Correct! 15% of 60 = 9, and 60 - 9 = 51. Next (harder): A price rises by 20% to £84. What was the original price?

Tips

  • Ask the AI to double-check its own working if something looks off.
  • For school work, ask it to use the same method your teacher uses.
