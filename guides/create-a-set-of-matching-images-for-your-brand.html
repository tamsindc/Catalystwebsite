Create a set of matching images for your brand

A reusable style recipe and a batch of AI-made images that all look like they belong together, for posts, blogs and slides.

Video, Image & VoiceBeginner20 minAI assistantCanva
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

When you need images and have no photos

Step by step

  1. Describe your look

    Note your brand colours, the mood you want and two or three images you like the style of (described in words, not copied).

  2. Write a style recipe

    Ask your AI assistant for a reusable style description, then use it for every image. ChatGPT and Gemini can create images directly; Canva's AI image tools work too.

    Prompt to copyCreate a reusable image style description for [business], whose brand feels [3 words] with colours [colours]. The style should suit [use: social posts, blog headers]. Give me: a one-paragraph style recipe (medium, lighting, colour palette, composition, level of detail, what to avoid). Then use it to create an image of: [first subject]. After that, I'll ask for more images using the same style.

  3. Make the rest in the same chat

    In the same conversation, ask for each new subject 'in the same style'. Keeping one chat helps the images stay consistent.

  4. Add text in Canva

    Bring the images into Canva to add headlines and your logo, so text is crisp and spelled correctly.

What you get

Style recipe: soft watercolour illustration, warm morning light, palette of sage green, cream and terracotta, simple compositions with lots of space, no text, no realistic faces. Images: a teapot on a windowsill; a bike by a garden gate; hands holding a seed packet.

Tips

  • Don't ask for images in the style of a named living artist or with real people's faces, and check your AI tool's terms for commercial use.
