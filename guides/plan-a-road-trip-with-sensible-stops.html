Plan a road trip with sensible stops

Get a road trip route broken into comfortable driving days, with breaks, overnight stops and things to see on the way.

TravelIntermediate25 minAI assistantGoogle Maps
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

A few weeks before a road trip

Step by step

  1. Set your limits

    Decide your start and end points, how many days you have and the most driving you want to do in a day.

  2. Ask for a route plan

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyPlan a road trip from [start] to [end] over [number] days for [who, including children or pets]. Maximum driving per day: [hours], with a break at least every [2] hours. We like [interests]. For each day give: route, approximate driving time, a good place for a break, one or two things to see, and an area to stay overnight. Note any toll roads, low-emission zones or ferry crossings I should check, and the best day to rest.

  3. Build the route in Google Maps

    Add each day's stops as a route with multiple destinations in Google Maps, and save the places to a list so they're on your phone.

  4. Check the rules

    Look up toll, clean-air zone and ferry details on official sites, as charges and rules change.

What you get

Day 1: Bristol to Portsmouth (2h10), break at a services near Salisbury, ferry evening crossing (book ahead). Day 2: Caen to Saint-Malo (2h30), walk the ramparts. Check: French toll roads, Crit'Air sticker for some cities.

Tips

  • Driving times from AI are estimates; Google Maps on the day will give you live traffic.
