Make a narrated explainer video in Canva

A short slideshow-style video with your own voiceover, captions and music, made in Canva from an AI-written script.

Video, Image & VoiceIntermediate45 minAI assistantCanvaPhone camera
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

When you need to explain something simply

Step by step

  1. Write a scene-by-scene script

    Paste this into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyWrite a script for a [60-90]-second explainer video about [topic] for [audience]. Split it into 6-8 scenes. For each scene: the voiceover (max 25 words, plain spoken English), a short on-screen headline (max 6 words), and a visual idea that I can make in Canva with icons, photos or simple shapes. End with a call to action: [CTA].

  2. Build the scenes in Canva

    Start a Canva video design, add one page per scene, and use templates, icons and your own photos. Add the headlines as big, readable text.

  3. Record the voiceover

    Use Canva's record option to narrate each page, or record on your phone's voice recorder in a quiet room and upload the audio. Your own voice builds more trust than a synthetic one.

  4. Add captions and music

    Turn on Canva's auto captions if offered on your plan, or type the voiceover as text. Pick a quiet track from Canva's audio library.

  5. Export and share

    Download as MP4 and upload to YouTube, your website or social channels.

What you get

Scene 1: 'Recycling bins confusing? You're not alone.' Visual: three coloured bins. Scene 3: 'Rinse it, squash it, lid on.' Visual: icon sequence. Scene 7: 'Download the bin calendar at our website.' Final: 75 seconds, captions on.

Tips

  • Only use music, photos and footage you have the rights to; Canva's library is licensed for use in your designs, subject to its terms.
  • If you include anyone else's face or voice, get their permission first.
