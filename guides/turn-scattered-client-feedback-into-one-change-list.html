Turn scattered client feedback into one change list

Collect comments from emails, calls and marked-up PDFs into a single numbered list of changes, with any conflicting requests flagged.

Graphic designerIntermediate20 minAI assistantGmailTrello
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

After every feedback round

Step by step

  1. Give each project a Gmail label

    Create a label for the project and set a Gmail filter so emails from the client's address land under it. Now all feedback is in one place.

  2. Gather everything for this round

    Copy the feedback emails, your notes from any calls and comments from marked-up PDFs into one document.

  3. Ask the AI to merge and sort it

    Paste it all into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere is all the feedback I've received on round [number] of a [type of design] from [number] people at my client: [paste feedback, with who said what]. Combine it into one numbered change list. For each item give: page or section, the change requested, who asked, and whether it is a must-fix, a preference or unclear. Then list separately: any requests that contradict each other, anything outside the original brief [paste brief deliverables], and questions I need to ask before I start. Keep wording neutral.

  4. Send it back to confirm

    Email the change list to your main contact and ask them to settle the conflicts and confirm before you begin. This stops endless extra rounds.

  5. Track it on a board

    Add each confirmed change as a card on a Trello board using its email-to-board address or by pasting, and tick them off as you go.

What you get

1. Cover - make logo larger (Sam, must-fix). 2. Page 3 - swap photo for one with people (Priya, preference). Conflicts: Sam wants navy background, Priya wants white. Outside brief: Priya asked for a matching email banner.

Tips

  • Remove personal phone numbers and email signatures before pasting.
  • Out-of-scope requests are a good moment to mention extra cost politely.
