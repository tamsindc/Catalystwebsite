Build a planned maintenance calendar for every building

A year-round maintenance plan for each property in a spreadsheet, with reminders in your calendar so checks and services never slip.

Property managerIntermediate45 minAI assistantGoogle SheetsGoogle Calendar
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Once a year, then follow the reminders

Step by step

  1. List what each building has

    Note the main systems and features for each property: boilers, lifts, fire alarms, emergency lighting, gutters, roofs, gardens, communal areas.

  2. Ask for a draft plan

    Paste the list with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyI manage [number] properties. For each, here are the main systems and features: [paste]. Create a planned maintenance schedule as a table: Property | Item | Task | How often | Suggested month | Who usually does it (contractor type) | Notes. Spread the work across the year so no month is overloaded. Mark tasks that are often legal or insurance requirements with [CHECK REQUIREMENT] so I confirm the rules that apply where I am ([country or state]).

  3. Confirm the legal checks

    Check the [CHECK REQUIREMENT] items against official guidance, your insurer and any compliance adviser, and adjust how often they happen.

  4. Put it in a sheet and calendar

    Paste the table into Google Sheets with a 'last done' column. Then add each task to Google Calendar as a recurring event with a two-week reminder.

What you get

Block A | Fire alarm | Weekly test | Weekly | All year | Caretaker | Log in fire book [CHECK REQUIREMENT] Block A | Gutters | Clear and inspect | Twice yearly | Apr, Nov | Roofing contractor Block B | Lift | Service | Every 6 months | Mar, Sep | Lift engineer

Tips

  • Add a 'last done' date and a link to each certificate so you can show evidence quickly if asked.
  • Review the plan each year and after any major repair.
