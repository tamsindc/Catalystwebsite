Get more from every trade show you attend

A plan for who to see before the show, quick notes on every conversation during it, and personal follow-ups sent within days.

When to use it

Before, during and after each trade show

Step by step

  1. Plan who to see

    Paste the exhibitor list or agenda into your AI assistant with a description of your ideal customer, and ask for a shortlist of stands and talks worth your time, with one opening question for each.

  2. Capture each conversation fast

    After each chat, record a 30-second voice note: who, what they need, how interested they are and what you promised. Snap a photo of their business card.

  3. Turn notes into a lead list

    Each evening, paste your voice note transcripts into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyI'm a sales rep at a trade show. Here are my notes from today's conversations: [paste transcripts]. Make a table with: Company, Contact role, What they need, Interest level (hot, warm, cold), What I promised, Next step, Follow-up date. Then write a short, personal follow-up email for each hot and warm lead that mentions something specific we talked about and includes the next step. Keep each email under 120 words.

  4. Follow up while it's fresh

    Paste the table into your Google Sheet, check each email, and send them within two working days of the show.

What you get

Harbour Fresh Foods | Purchasing lead | Needs a cheaper chilled packaging option | Hot | Promised samples of the 2L tray | Send samples and book a call | 18 Nov.

Tips

  • Say the company name clearly at the start of each voice note. It makes the transcript much easier to sort later.
