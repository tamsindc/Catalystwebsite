Check your rental adverts are fair to everyone

Get your rental adverts checked for wording that could put people off or exclude them unfairly, with fairer alternatives ready to use.

Property managerBeginner5 minAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Before any rental advert goes live

Step by step

  1. Paste your draft advert

    Copy the advert text, including any 'requirements' section.

  2. Ask for a fairness check

    Paste it into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyReview this rental advert for wording that could discriminate against, or put off, people because of characteristics protected by law where I am ([country or state]), such as race, religion, sex, disability, age, family status or receiving benefits. For each issue: quote the wording, explain in one line why it could be a problem, and suggest a fairer alternative that describes the property or the objective requirement instead. Then check the advert is clear on rent, deposit, fees and accessibility features. Advert: [paste]

  3. Check with your compliance adviser

    The AI can miss local rules or flag things that are fine. If you're unsure, check with your compliance team or a legal adviser before publishing.

  4. Save a fair template

    Keep the corrected advert as a template so future adverts start fair.

What you get

Issue: 'Ideal for young professionals' could put off older applicants. Try: 'Close to the station and city centre.' Issue: 'No children' may be unlawful where you are. Try describing the property, e.g. 'One-bedroom flat, suits one or two people.'

Tips

  • Describe the home and the objective requirements, such as rent and references, not the kind of person you want.
  • This is a checking aid, not legal advice. Rules differ by country and state.
