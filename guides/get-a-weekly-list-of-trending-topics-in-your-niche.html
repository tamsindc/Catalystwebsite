Get a weekly list of trending topics in your niche

Every Monday you get five timely post ideas based on what people are talking about in your field, logged in a sheet for planning.

When to use it

Every Monday morning

Step by step

  1. Set up a scheduled task

    In ChatGPT, if your plan includes scheduled tasks, ask it to run a task every Monday at 8am with the prompt below. It will send you the results as a notification or email.

    Prompt to copySearch the web for what's being discussed this week in [your niche] that matters to [audience] in [country]. Look at news, Google Trends topics and popular questions. Give me 5 post ideas as a table: Topic, Why it's timely (one line, with the source link), Post angle for [business type], Best platform, Suggested first line. Skip anything older than 10 days, and skip sensitive tragedies.

  2. Have a free fallback ready

    No scheduled tasks? Create a recurring Monday event in Google Calendar called 'Trend check' with the prompt pasted in the description, and run it yourself in any AI assistant with web search.

  3. Log ideas in a sheet

    Make a Google Sheet called Trend ideas with the same columns plus Date and Used. Paste each week's table in so you build a bank of ideas over time.

  4. Check before you post

    Click the source link for any idea you use. Trends move fast and AI can misread dates.

What you get

Topic: New rules on garden water butts | Timely: council grants announced Tuesday (link) | Angle: 'How to claim yours in 3 steps' | Instagram carousel | First line: 'Free water butt, anyone?'

Tips

  • Use Google Trends' Explore page to compare two topics quickly before you commit to one.
