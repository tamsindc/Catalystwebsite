Run one-to-ones your team look forward to

A simple, repeatable agenda for regular one-to-ones, tailored questions for each person and a short record of what you both agreed.

HR & HiringBeginner15 minAI assistantGoogle CalendarGoogle Docs
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Before each regular one-to-one

Step by step

  1. Book a regular slot

    Set a recurring Google Calendar event, such as 30 minutes every fortnight, with each team member, and attach a shared Google Doc for notes.

  2. Get an agenda and questions

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyCreate a 30-minute one-to-one agenda for me (a manager at a [type of business]) and a [role] who has been with us [time]. What I know: recent wins [wins], current challenges [challenges], their goals [goals]. Include: a 5-minute check-in, their topics first, progress on goals, one development question, any feedback both ways, and agreed actions. Suggest 6 open questions I could rotate over the next few months, such as about workload, learning and what support they need. Supportive tone, British English.

  3. Let them add topics first

    Paste the agenda into the shared doc a couple of days before and ask them to add what they'd like to talk about at the top.

  4. Record actions together

    At the end, write down two or three agreed actions with names and dates in the doc, so you can check them next time.

What you get

Agenda, 14 May: 1. How are things? (5 min) 2. Ellis's topics: training budget, Friday cover 3. Goal: lead the stock count, on track 4. Question: 'What would make next month easier?' 5. Feedback both ways. Actions: Ellis books first-aid course by 31 May; I sort Friday cover rota.

Tips

  • Keep one-to-ones even when you're busy; cancelling them often sends the wrong message.
  • Keep notes respectful and factual, as the team member can see them and may ask for them later.
