Win better shelf space with a store-first pitch

A short, store-specific pitch showing why better placement or a promotion for your products is good for the retailer, not just for you.

Sales repBeginner15 minAI assistantGoogle Docs
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Before a meeting with a store or buyer

Step by step

  1. Gather your facts

    Note what you know: your sales in that store or similar stores, what's selling well, upcoming promotions or launches, and where your products sit now.

  2. Ask for a retailer-first pitch

    Paste the facts into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyI'm a sales rep for [brand or product range] meeting the buyer at [type of store]. Facts: [paste sales figures, current placement, upcoming promotions, customer trends you've seen]. Write a one-page pitch asking for [better shelf position / an end display / a joint promotion]. Lead with what's in it for the store: footfall, basket size, margin or fewer gaps. Include: the ask, three reasons with my facts, what we'll provide (for example display materials or staff training), a simple way to measure success, and two likely buyer objections with answers. Only use the figures I've given.

  3. Rehearse the objections

    Ask your AI assistant to play a sceptical buyer and push back on your pitch, so you've practised your answers before the meeting.

What you get

Ask: move our granola range from the bottom shelf to eye level for 8 weeks. Why: it's already your third-best seller in the aisle; launch ad campaign starts in March. We provide: shelf strips and a tasting day. Measure: weekly units versus the last 8 weeks.

Tips

  • Bring one or two numbers the buyer can check in their own system. They'll trust those more than anything else.
