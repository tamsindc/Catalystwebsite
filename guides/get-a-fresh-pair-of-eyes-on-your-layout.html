Get a fresh pair of eyes on your layout

Upload a screenshot of your draft and get a structured critique on hierarchy, spacing, readability and typos before the client sees it.

Graphic designerBeginner5 minAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Before sending any draft to a client

Step by step

  1. Export a clear image of the draft

    Save the layout as a PNG or JPG at a size where the text is readable. For multi-page pieces, export the pages you want checked most.

  2. Ask for a structured critique

    Upload the image to your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyAct as a senior art director reviewing this draft [format] for [client type and goal]. The main message the viewer must get is: [main message]. Review it under these headings: 1. What the eye sees first, second and third, and whether that order is right. 2. Spacing and alignment issues. 3. Readability of text at real size. 4. Colour and contrast, including accessibility. 5. Any typos or inconsistent wording you can see. 6. The 3 changes that would improve it most, in order. Be specific about where on the page each issue is.

  3. Decide what to change

    Treat the critique as a second opinion, not an instruction. Fix what you agree with and note anything you deliberately kept.

  4. Proofread the words separately

    Paste the copy as text and ask the AI to list spelling and grammar issues only, since reading text from images is not always perfect.

What you get

1. Eye goes to the photo, then the price, then the headline. The headline should come before the price. 2. Left margin on the text box is tighter than the image above. 3. Footer text looks under 7pt. 6. Top fix: enlarge headline and move the price below it.

Tips

  • Do not upload unreleased client work if your contract or client forbids it; blur names or products if needed.
  • Ask the same questions about your previous version to check the changes actually helped.
