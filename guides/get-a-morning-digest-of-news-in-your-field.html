Get a morning digest of news in your field

A short daily briefing with the five stories that matter for your job or industry, each summarised in two lines with a link.

Content & WritingIntermediate10 minAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every weekday morning

Step by step

  1. Decide what you want to follow

    List your industry, two or three topics, any sources you trust, and what you want to skip.

  2. Create a scheduled task

    In ChatGPT, if your plan includes scheduled tasks, ask it to run this every weekday at 7.30am. It will notify you when the digest is ready. Gemini also offers scheduled actions on some plans.

    Prompt to copyEvery weekday, search for the most important news from the last 24 hours about [industry/topics] relevant to [your role] in [country]. Prefer [trusted sources]. Skip [topics to ignore]. Give me 5 items: headline, two-line summary, why it matters to someone in my role, and the link. Then one line: 'Worth writing about:' with a content idea if any story suits my audience of [audience].

  3. Test it and tighten it

    Read the first few digests and reply with changes like 'fewer big-company stories, more UK small business'. Update the task's prompt to match.

  4. No scheduled tasks? Use a fallback

    Save the prompt in your notes app and run it each morning in any AI assistant with web search, or set up Google Alerts for your topics.

What you get

1. New rules on cookie banners from 1 Jan - affects every small business website; check your site's consent pop-up. (link) 2. Card fees cut for small retailers... Worth writing about: 'What the new cookie rules mean for your shop website.'

Tips

  • Click through before sharing any story; summaries can miss nuance or mix up dates.
