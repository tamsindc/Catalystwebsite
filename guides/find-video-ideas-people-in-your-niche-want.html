When to use it

When planning your next batch of videos

Step by step

  1. Collect YouTube search suggestions

    Type your topic into the YouTube search bar slowly and note the suggestions. Try adding 'how to', 'best', 'for beginners' and 'vs'. Also note titles of popular recent videos in your niche.

  2. Ask the AI to turn them into ideas

    Paste what you found into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyMy YouTube channel covers [topic] for [audience]. Here are YouTube search suggestions and popular video titles I found: [paste]. Give me 20 video ideas as a table: Working title (under 60 characters), The question it answers, My angle (how I can make it different given [your experience or style]), Format (tutorial, review, story, list), Difficulty to film (easy/medium/hard). Group them into 3-4 themes. Don't copy existing titles.

  3. Pick three to film

    Choose the easiest-to-film ideas from your strongest theme and add them to your calendar.

What you get

Tips

  • Check YouTube Studio's Research tab, if your channel has it, to see what your own viewers are searching for.
