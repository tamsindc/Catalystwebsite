Share a weekly market watch with your team

Gather industry news from alerts and newsletters into one place, then post a short weekly market summary to your team's Slack channel.

Research & NewsAdvanced40 minGmailAI assistantSlackGoogle Calendar
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every Monday morning

Step by step

  1. Set up your news sources

    Create Google Alerts for your industry, key customers and competitors, delivered weekly. Subscribe to two or three trusted trade newsletters.

  2. Route them to one label

    In Gmail's settings, under Filters, send all of these to a 'Market watch' label and skip the inbox. Outlook users can do the same with a rule and folder.

  3. Block 20 minutes on Mondays

    Add a recurring Monday event called 'Market watch' so it becomes a habit, and invite a colleague as backup.

  4. Draft the summary

    Copy the week's emails into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyWrite a team market-watch post from the news below for a [industry] business. Format for Slack: a bold one-line headline, then 'Need to know' (3 bullets max), 'Worth watching' (3 bullets max) and 'Customers and competitors' (any mentions). Each bullet: one sentence, source name and link. Keep it under 200 words, neutral in tone, and leave out anything without a link. [paste emails]

  5. Check, then post

    Open each link to confirm it's accurate and current, then post in your team channel (Microsoft Teams works the same way). Start a thread for discussion.

What you get

*Market watch, 10 Nov: steel prices ease for a second month* Need to know: steel rebar down 3% (Builders Weekly, link). New site safety guidance published (link). Worth watching: two regional rivals advertising for estimators (link).

Tips

  • Ask colleagues to react with a thumbs up to items they found useful, and use that to tune your alerts.
