Turn receipt photos into a tidy expenses sheet

Snap receipts as you go and turn them into a spreadsheet with date, supplier, amount, VAT and category, ready for your bookkeeper.

Small Business AdminIntermediate15 minPhone cameraAI assistantGoogle Sheets
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Weekly, or whenever receipts pile up

Step by step

  1. Photograph receipts straight away

    Lay each receipt flat in good light and take a photo. Keep them in one phone album or a Drive folder called 'Receipts'.

  2. Set up your expenses sheet

    Create a Google Sheet with columns: Date, Supplier, Description, Net, VAT, Total, Category, Payment method.

  3. Extract the details

    Once a week, upload the photos to your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyExtract the details from each receipt photo into a table with columns: Date (DD/MM/YYYY), Supplier, Description, Net, VAT, Total, Suggested category (choose from: [your categories, e.g. Materials, Fuel, Travel, Office, Software, Meals]), Payment method. Copy amounts exactly as printed. If VAT isn't shown separately, leave it blank rather than calculating it. Add a 'Check' column and write 'yes' for any receipt that's blurry or unclear.

  4. Paste and check

    Paste the table into your sheet and check totals against the photos, especially any marked 'Check'.

  5. Keep the originals

    Keep the receipt photos (and paper copies if needed) for as long as your tax authority requires. Your accountant can tell you how long.

Tips

  • Categories are suggestions only. Ask your accountant which categories and VAT treatment apply to your business.
  • If you use Xero or QuickBooks, their mobile apps can capture receipts directly, which may suit you better.
