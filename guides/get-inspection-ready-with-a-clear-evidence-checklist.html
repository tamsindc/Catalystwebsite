Get inspection-ready with a clear evidence checklist

Build a checklist of what an inspector is likely to ask for, mapped to your documents and owners, so gaps are fixed before anyone arrives.

When to use it

When an inspection or external audit is announced

Step by step

  1. Collect what you know

    Gather the inspection notice, the standard or rules being inspected against, and last time's findings.

  2. Draft the evidence checklist

    Attach these to your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyWe have a [type of inspection] by [regulator or body] on [date], against [standard or rules]. Last inspection findings: [paste]. Create an evidence checklist as a table: Requirement or area | Evidence the inspector is likely to ask for | Our document or record (leave blank for me) | Owner | Ready? (Yes / No / Partial) | Action and due date. Group by area. Add a separate list of likely interview questions for staff, and highlight anything linked to last time's findings. Don't assume we comply; this is a checklist to fill in.

  3. Fill in your side

    Copy the table into Excel on OneDrive, add your own document references and owners, and check the requirements against the actual standard.

  4. Close the gaps

    Book short check-ins in Outlook Calendar with owners each week until the inspection, and update the Ready column as items are fixed.

  5. Brief the team

    Ask the AI to turn the staff questions into a one-page briefing note, then review it so it's accurate and honest about how you work.

What you get

Training records | Signed training logs for all line staff in last 12 months | TR-2024 log | J. Patel | Partial | Chase 3 night-shift staff by 14 Feb. Repeat finding: calibration records for scales | Certificates for all 6 scales | CAL folder | M. Osei | No | Book calibration by 10 Feb.

Tips

  • Never coach staff to give scripted answers; the briefing is to help them find and explain the real evidence.
  • Don't paste confidential submissions, customer details or personal data into a public AI tool unless your organisation allows it.
