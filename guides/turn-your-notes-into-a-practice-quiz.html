Turn your notes into a practice quiz

Paste or photograph your notes and get a ten-question quiz with answers, so you can test yourself instead of just re-reading.

LearningBeginner5 minAI assistantPhone camera
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

After each study session

Step by step

  1. Gather your notes

    Copy your typed notes, or take clear photos of handwritten pages or textbook sections.

  2. Ask for a quiz

    Open your AI assistant, add the notes and paste this prompt.

    Prompt to copyUsing only the notes below, write a 10-question quiz to help me revise [topic] for [exam or level]. Mix the types: 4 multiple choice, 3 short answer, 2 'explain in your own words' and 1 question that applies the idea to a new example. Don't show the answers yet. Ask me the questions one at a time, wait for my answer, then tell me if I'm right, explain briefly, and move on. At the end, list the topics I got wrong. [paste notes]

  3. Answer honestly

    Type your answers without looking at your notes. Getting things wrong now is how your memory improves.

  4. Save the weak spots

    Copy the list of topics you got wrong into your notes app and start your next session with them.

What you get

Q3 (short answer): What is the role of chlorophyll in photosynthesis? You said: 'it makes the plant green'. Partly right: it absorbs light energy, mostly red and blue, which powers the reaction. Weak spots today: limiting factors, the word equation.

Tips

  • Ask it to stick to your notes so it doesn't test you on things your course doesn't cover.
  • Check any surprising answer against your textbook, as AI can occasionally get facts wrong.
