When to use it

A few weeks before a trip

Step by step

  1. Jot down the basics

    Note where you're staying, your dates, arrival and departure times, who's going and what you enjoy.

  2. Ask for a paced itinerary

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyPlan a [number]-day trip to [destination] for [who is going]. We arrive [day and time] and leave [day and time], staying near [area or hotel]. We enjoy [interests] and prefer a [relaxed/moderate/packed] pace with [amount] of walking. For each day give: morning, afternoon and evening, grouped by area to cut travel time, how to get between places, one rainy-day alternative, and a rough daily spend. Mark anything that usually needs booking ahead.

  3. Check the details

    Look up each place on its official website for current opening times, prices and booking rules, as these change often.

  4. Pin it to a map

    Save each stop to a Google Maps list for the trip so you can see what's nearby while you're there.

What you get

Day 2 (Old Town): 9:30 castle (book ahead, allow 2h), lunch near the square, 2pm river walk, 4pm rest at hotel, 7pm dinner in the market district. Rainy day: national museum. Rough spend: 60 for two.

Tips

  • AI can suggest places that have closed or changed hours, so always check official sites before you go.
