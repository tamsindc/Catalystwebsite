Pack school lunches your fussy eater will actually eat

Get two weeks of varied lunchbox ideas built around your child's safe foods, with a few gentle new things to try.

Food & HomeBeginner5 minAI assistantNotes app
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every couple of weeks during term

Step by step

  1. List what your child will eat

    Write down their 'safe' foods, things they've refused, any allergies and whether the school is nut-free.

  2. Ask for a lunchbox rotation

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyMy child is [age] and a fussy eater. They reliably eat: [list]. They refuse: [list]. Allergies or school rules: [e.g. nut-free school]. Create a 10-day packed lunch rotation that mostly uses their safe foods, with one small new or slightly different item each day to try. Include a main, a fruit or veg, and a snack, no reheating needed, and keep prep under [10] minutes in the morning. Add a short list of things I can prep on Sunday.

  3. Keep the rotation handy

    Save it in your notes app and add the shopping items to your list.

What you get

Day 1: ham & cheese pinwheels, cucumber sticks, apple slices; try: 3 cherry tomatoes. Day 2: pasta salad with sweetcorn, grapes (halved), plain popcorn; try: a pepper strip... Sunday prep: cook pasta, wash grapes, bake mini muffins.

Tips

  • Halve grapes and cherry tomatoes for younger children, and always check your school's food policy.
