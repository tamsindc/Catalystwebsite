Pack the right things for this trip's weather

Get a packing list tailored to your destination, activities, luggage allowance and who's travelling.

When to use it

About a week before you leave

Step by step

  1. Check the forecast and allowance

    Look up the weather forecast for your destination and your airline's baggage allowance on their official site.

  2. Ask for a tailored list

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyMake a packing list for [number] nights in [destination] in [month]. Expected weather: [forecast]. Travellers: [adults, children's ages]. Activities: [e.g. beach, hiking, one smart dinner]. Luggage: [e.g. cabin bag only, 10kg]. Laundry available: [yes/no]. Group the list by person and category (clothes, toiletries, documents, tech, health, kids' stuff), give quantities, and suggest outfits that mix and match. Flag liquids rules for hand luggage to check on the airport's site.

  3. Save it as a checklist

    Paste the list into your notes app as a checklist and tick items off as you pack. Keep it for next time.

What you get

Each adult: 4 tops, 2 shorts, 1 trousers, light rain jacket, swimwear x2. Documents: passports, insurance details, booking printouts. Kids: 2 changes in hand luggage, sun hats, travel games. Tech: adapter (type F), power bank in hand luggage.

Tips

  • Liquid and battery rules differ by airport and airline, so check their official sites before packing hand luggage.
