Get a morning news briefing on topics you choose

Set up a daily briefing from your AI assistant that rounds up news on your chosen topics, with links so you can read further.

Research & NewsIntermediate10 minAI assistantReminders app
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every weekday at 7am

Step by step

  1. Pick three to five topics

    Be specific: 'electric van grants in the UK' works better than 'cars'. Write them in a note so you can tweak them later.

  2. Create a scheduled task

    If your plan includes it, ChatGPT's scheduled tasks and Gemini's scheduled actions can run a prompt for you each day and notify you. Paste this prompt and set the time.

    Prompt to copyEvery weekday, search the news from the last 24 hours on these topics: [topic 1], [topic 2], [topic 3]. For each topic give up to 3 stories as: headline, one-sentence summary, why it matters to [who I am, e.g. a small café owner], publisher, date and link. Skip opinion pieces unless clearly labelled. If there is no real news on a topic, say 'nothing new' rather than filling space.

  3. Free fallback

    No scheduled tasks? Set a recurring 7am reminder in your Reminders app and paste the same prompt into an assistant with web search each morning.

  4. Click before you quote

    Treat the briefing as a pointer, not the final word. Open the link before you share or act on any story, as dates and details can be wrong.

What you get

Café trade: 1) Milk prices expected to rise 4p a litre (Farming Weekly, 9 Nov, link). Why it matters: your latte margin. 2) Council opens applications for outdoor seating permits (Local Gazette, 8 Nov, link). Energy grants: nothing new.

Tips

  • After a week, reply with which stories were useful and which weren't, and ask it to adjust.
