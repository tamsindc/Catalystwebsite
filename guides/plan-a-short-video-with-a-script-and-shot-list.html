Plan a short video with a script and shot list

A 30 to 60 second script with a strong hook, plus a simple shot list you can film on your phone in one go.

Video, Image & VoiceBeginner10 minAI assistantPhone camera
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Before filming any Reel, Short or TikTok

Step by step

  1. Decide the one point

    Write down the single thing you want viewers to learn or feel. Short videos work best with just one idea.

  2. Get a script and shot list

    Paste this into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyWrite a [30/45/60]-second vertical video script for [business or channel] aimed at [audience]. Main point: [point]. Format: 1) a hook for the first 2 seconds, spoken and as on-screen text; 2) the script split into numbered shots, each with what's said, what's on screen, and how long it lasts; 3) on-screen captions in short phrases; 4) a closing line with a call to action: [CTA]; 5) a caption for posting under 60 words. Everything must be filmable on a phone by one person at [location].

  3. Film shot by shot

    Film each shot separately in good natural light, holding the phone upright. Do two takes of each so you have a spare.

  4. Edit and add captions

    Put the clips together in your phone's editor, Instagram, YouTube Shorts or Canva, and turn on automatic captions where offered; most viewers watch with the sound off.

What you get

Hook: 'You're watering your houseplants wrong.' Shot 1 (2s): close-up of wilting plant. Shot 2 (5s): finger in soil, 'Check two knuckles deep first.' ... Shot 5 (4s): healthy plant, 'Follow for one plant tip a week.'

Tips

  • Ask anyone who appears in your video for permission first, and never film customers without asking.
