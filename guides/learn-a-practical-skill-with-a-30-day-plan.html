When to use it

When you start learning something new

Step by step

  1. Pick one skill and a goal

    Be specific: not 'learn guitar' but 'play three songs with basic chords'.

  2. Ask for a plan

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyCreate a 30-day plan for me to learn [skill]. My goal by day 30: [goal]. My starting level: [describe]. I can spend [minutes] a day, [days] a week. Equipment I have: [list]. Break it into 4 weekly themes. For each day, give: one small task, how long it takes, and what kind of free resource to search for (for example 'search YouTube for beginner G chord tutorial'). Include a short practice log question each day, and a mini test at the end of each week.

  3. Find your resources

    Search YouTube or your library app using the suggestions. Don't rely on the AI for specific video links, as they can be out of date.

  4. Schedule practice

    Add a recurring Google Calendar event at your practice time, with the day's task in the description.

  5. Check in weekly

    At the end of each week, tell the AI how it went and ask it to adjust the next week.

What you get

Week 1: Basics. Day 1 (15 min): Learn to hold the guitar and tune it. Search: 'beginner guitar tuning app tutorial'. Log: what felt awkward? Day 2: Learn E minor chord... Week 1 test: switch between Em and G 10 times in a minute.

Tips

  • Short daily practice beats one long weekend session.
  • If you miss a day, just carry on. Ask the AI to compress the plan if needed.
