Declutter a room in short, doable sessions

Break a cluttered room into 20-minute sessions with clear keep, donate and bin decisions so you actually finish.

Food & HomeBeginner5 minAI assistantPhone camera
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

When a room feels overwhelming

Step by step

  1. Photograph the room

    Take two or three wide photos showing the problem areas, like the wardrobe, desk or garage shelves.

  2. Ask for a session plan

    Attach the photos to your AI assistant and paste this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere are photos of my [room]. I can spend [20] minutes at a time, [number] times a week. Break decluttering this room into short sessions, starting with the quickest visible win. For each session give: the exact area (for example 'top shelf only'), what to sort, simple rules for keep, donate, sell or bin, and what 'done' looks like. Include a final session for taking donations out of the house.

  3. Book the sessions in

    Put the sessions in your calendar or reminders app like appointments, and tick each one off.

What you get

Session 1 (20m): bedside table only. Bin anything broken, keep 1 book. Session 2: top wardrobe shelf - donate anything not worn in a year... Session 6: drop the donate bags at a charity shop, no second-guessing.

Tips

  • Keep a 'donate' bag by the door and take it out the same week so things don't creep back.
