Save towards a goal with a clear weekly plan

A saving plan for a holiday, deposit or big purchase that tells you how much to put aside each week and tracks your progress.

When to use it

When you start saving for something specific

Step by step

  1. Define the goal

    Decide what you're saving for, how much it will cost, and the date you need it by.

  2. Ask for a plan

    Share the goal and your rough budget with your AI assistant using this prompt.

    Prompt to copyI want to save [amount] for [goal] by [date]. I've already saved [amount]. After bills, I usually have about [amount] spare each month. Work out how much I need to save per week and per month. If that's not realistic with my spare money, show two alternatives: a later date, or a smaller target. Give me 5 practical, low-effort ways to find the extra money based on everyday spending. Then create a simple weekly tracker table with columns: Week, Date, Planned, Actually saved, Running total, which I can paste into Google Sheets.

  3. Set up the tracker

    Paste the tracker into a Google Sheet. Many banking apps let you create a separate savings pot, so you can move the weekly amount there.

  4. Set a weekly nudge

    Add a recurring calendar reminder or phone reminder on payday to move the money and update the sheet.

What you get

Goal: £1,200 summer holiday by 1 July (33 weeks). Saved: £150. Need: £32 a week (£139 a month). Ideas: swap two takeaways a month (£40), sell unused gadgets (one-off £80), pause one streaming service (£11). Week 1 | 16 Nov | £32 | | £150

Tips

  • Automating the transfer on payday works far better than relying on willpower.
  • This is a planning aid, not advice on where to keep your savings. Check options with a qualified adviser for larger sums.
