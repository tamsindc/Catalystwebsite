Reply to Instagram comments and messages faster

A bank of warm, ready-made replies for the questions you get most, saved in Instagram so you can answer in two taps.

Marketing & SocialBeginner15 minAI assistantInstagram
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Set up once, refresh every few months

Step by step

  1. List your most common questions

    Scroll back through your comments and DMs and write down the ten questions you answer most, like prices, opening hours or delivery.

  2. Draft replies with AI

    Paste the list into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyI run [business] on Instagram. Here are the questions people ask most: [list]. For each, write a short reply (under 40 words) that sounds friendly and human, answers the question using these facts: [prices, hours, links, policies], and ends with a light next step. Also give each reply a 1-2 word shortcut name. Format: Shortcut | Reply. British English, no emoji overload (one at most).

  3. Save them as quick replies

    In Instagram, professional accounts can save replies for messages: look in your settings under Business or Creator tools for saved or quick replies, then add each shortcut and reply.

  4. Personalise before sending

    When you use one, add the person's name or a detail from their message so it never feels copied and pasted.

What you get

price | Hi! Our cut and finish starts at £38, and colour from £65. Want me to check this week's free slots for you? delivery | We post UK-wide in 2-3 working days, free over £40. Anything I can help you choose?

Tips

  • Never paste customers' private messages into an AI tool with their names or phone numbers; the questions alone are enough.
