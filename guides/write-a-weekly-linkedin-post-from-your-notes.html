Write a weekly LinkedIn post from your notes

Keep a running list of thoughts in Notion and turn the best one into a polished LinkedIn post every week, scheduled ahead.

Marketing & SocialIntermediate20 minNotionAI assistantLinkedIn
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every Monday morning

Step by step

  1. Set up an ideas page in Notion

    Create a Notion database called LinkedIn ideas with columns for Idea, Story or example, and Status. Add anything that comes up during your week.

  2. Pick one idea and draft it

    Copy your chosen idea and example into your AI assistant with this prompt. If your Notion plan includes Notion AI you can run it right inside the page.

    Prompt to copyWrite a LinkedIn post from this idea: [idea]. Real example from my work: [example]. I am a [job title] who helps [audience]. Structure: a one-line hook that states a surprising point; the story in 3-4 short paragraphs; one practical lesson readers can use today; a closing question. 150-220 words. No emoji, no hashtags in the body, up to 3 hashtags at the end. British English, plain and warm, no buzzwords.

  3. Edit it into your own words

    Change at least one sentence so it sounds like you speaking. Remove anything that names a client without their permission.

  4. Schedule it on LinkedIn

    When writing a post on LinkedIn, use the clock icon to schedule it for a time your audience is online, such as Tuesday mid-morning. Mark the idea as Posted in Notion.

What you get

Hook: 'The best proposal I ever wrote was one page long.' Story: a client who skimmed a 20-page deck... Lesson: lead with the decision you need from them. Question: 'What's the shortest document that ever won you work?' #consulting #writing

Tips

  • Keep client stories anonymous unless you have written permission to name them.
