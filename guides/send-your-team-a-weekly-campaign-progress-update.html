Send your team a weekly campaign progress update

Every Monday, turn your campaign spreadsheet into a short update showing money raised against target, what's working and what needs a push.

FundraiserAdvanced45 minAI assistantGoogle SheetsGmailGoogle Calendar
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every Monday during a campaign

Step by step

  1. Set up a campaign tracker

    Make a Google Sheet with tabs for income by week and by channel (online, events, post, corporate), the target, costs so far, and a notes tab for activity like mailings sent or events held.

  2. Book a weekly slot

    Create a recurring Monday morning Google Calendar event to update the sheet. If your ChatGPT plan includes scheduled tasks, set one to remind you with the prompt below.

  3. Write the update from the numbers

    Copy the week's totals and notes into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyWrite a weekly fundraising update for [campaign] for my team and [trustees / manager]. Target: [amount] by [date]. Raised so far: [amount]. This week by channel: [paste]. Last week by channel: [paste]. Costs to date: [amount]. Activity this week: [paste notes]. Include: a one-line headline, percentage of target reached and weeks remaining, what rose or fell compared with last week and the likely reason based on the notes, the best-performing channel, cost to raise each pound so far, 3 suggested focus areas for this week, and anything that needs a decision. Under 250 words. Use only these figures and show your sums.

  4. Check the sums and send

    Check the percentages against your sheet, then email the update from Gmail to the team or post it in your usual team chat.

  5. Keep a running record

    Paste each update into a single Google Doc so you can see the whole campaign story and use it in your final report.

What you get

Headline: 62% of target with 4 weeks to go. Raised £18,600 of £30,000. Online up £1,200 on last week after Thursday's email; events flat. Cost to raise £1: 18p. Focus: second email to lapsed supporters, confirm two corporate matches. Decision needed: extend quiz night ticket sales?

Tips

  • Keep donor names out of the tracker you share with the AI; weekly totals by channel are enough.
  • Financial decisions about the campaign should still be checked with your finance lead.
