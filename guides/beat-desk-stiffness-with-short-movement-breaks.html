Beat desk stiffness with short movement breaks

Get a set of two-minute stretches you can do at your desk, with gentle reminders through the working day.

Health & FitnessBeginner5 minAI assistantReminders app
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

During long days at a desk

Step by step

  1. Ask for a stretch routine

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyI sit at a desk for about [hours] a day and feel stiff in my [neck/shoulders/back/hips]. Create 4 different 2-minute movement breaks I can do at my desk or next to it, in work clothes, without getting sweaty. For each: 3-4 moves with simple instructions, how long to hold or repeat, and an easier option. Tell me to stop any move that causes pain.

  2. Set reminders

    Create repeating reminders every 60 to 90 minutes during work hours on your phone or computer, each named after one of the routines.

  3. Pair it with a habit

    Link a break to something you already do, like after every video call or each time you refill your water.

What you get

Break 1 - Neck & shoulders: shoulder rolls x10 back, x10 forward. Slow chin tucks x8. Ear-to-shoulder hold 15 sec each side. Doorway chest stretch 20 sec. Easier: do it all seated.

Tips

  • If pain persists or you have numbness or tingling, see your GP or a physiotherapist.
