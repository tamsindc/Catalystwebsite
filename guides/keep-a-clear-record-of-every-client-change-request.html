Keep a clear record of every client change request

Collect client change requests from email into one log with what changed, cost and time impact, and who approved it.

Construction managerIntermediate35 minGmailAI assistantGoogle Sheets
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Whenever a client, architect or engineer asks for a change

Step by step

  1. Label change requests in your inbox

    In Gmail, create a 'Variations' label and set up a filter for emails from the client and design team that mention words like change, instruction or variation. Label others by hand as they arrive.

  2. Set up the log

    Make a Google Sheet with columns: reference, date requested, requested by, description, reason, cost impact, time impact, status (pending, approved, rejected), approved by and date approved.

  3. Pull the details out with AI

    Once a week, paste the new labelled emails into your AI assistant with this prompt and paste the result into the log.

    Prompt to copyExtract every change request from the emails below. For each give: date, who asked, a one-line description, the reason given, any cost or time figures mentioned (write 'not stated' if none), and whether written approval has been given yet. Output a table with columns: date, requested by, description, reason, cost impact, time impact, approval status. Don't guess values that aren't in the emails. Emails: [paste]

  4. Confirm in writing

    For anything still pending, send the client a short email confirming the change, its cost and time effect and asking for written approval before work starts.

What you get

Tips

  • The log supports your contract process but doesn't replace it; follow the variation procedure in your contract and get legal or commercial advice on disputes.
