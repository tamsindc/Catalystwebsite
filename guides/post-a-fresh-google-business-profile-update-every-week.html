Post a fresh Google Business Profile update every week

Short, local updates for your Google Business Profile that keep your listing active and tell nearby customers what's new.

Marketing & SocialBeginner10 minAI assistantPhone camera
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every week

Step by step

  1. Take one photo

    Snap a recent job, product, dish or your team at work. Real photos tend to do better than stock images.

  2. Write four updates at once

    Paste this into your AI assistant to get a month's worth.

    Prompt to copyWrite 4 Google Business Profile updates for [business] in [town]. What's happening this month: [news, offers, events, seasonal services]. Each update: 60-100 words, mentions the local area naturally, describes one specific thing (a job, offer or event), and ends with a clear action that matches a button: Call, Book, Learn more or Order online. Avoid phone numbers and links in the text. British English, friendly and plain.

  3. Post it on your profile

    Search your business name on Google or open the Google Business Profile app while signed in, choose to add an update, add the photo and text, and pick the matching button.

What you get

'This week we fitted a new walk-in shower for a family in Headingley, swapping a tired bath for something far easier to use. If you're thinking about a bathroom refresh before spring, we're booking free home visits for February. Tap Book to choose a time.'

Tips

  • Ask the homeowner before posting photos of their house, and leave out house numbers.
