Update your procedures when the rules change

Get a marked-up version of an existing procedure showing exactly what to change to meet a new requirement, with a short change log.

Compliance officerBeginner20 minAI assistantWordGoogle Docs
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

After a rule change or audit finding

Step by step

  1. Gather the procedure and the change

    Open the current procedure and the new rule, guidance or audit finding that it needs to reflect.

  2. Ask for a gap check and redraft

    Paste both into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere is our current procedure, [title and number], and the new requirement it must meet. 1) List the gaps: Section of procedure | What the requirement says | What our procedure says now | Change needed. 2) Rewrite only the sections that need changing, keeping our existing style, numbering and headings. Mark new text in [square brackets]. 3) Write a short change log entry (what changed and why). Don't change anything that isn't affected. PROCEDURE: [paste] NEW REQUIREMENT: [paste]

  3. Review with the process owner

    Walk through the changes with the people who do the work, to check the new steps are practical, and get legal or quality sign-off as your system requires.

  4. Publish through your normal control process

    Update the version number, add the change log and release it through your document control system so old copies are withdrawn.

What you get

Gap: s.5.3 Complaint logging. Requirement: log within 1 working day. Ours: 'as soon as practical'. Change: set a 1-day limit. New text: [All complaints must be logged in the complaints system within one working day of receipt.] Change log: v4.1, s.5.3 updated.

Tips

  • Ask it to suggest two or three quiz questions on the change; they're useful for read-and-understand records.
  • Always compare the redraft with the original yourself; AI can quietly drop a line it thought wasn't needed.
