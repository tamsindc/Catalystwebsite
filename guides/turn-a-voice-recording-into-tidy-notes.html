Turn a voice recording into tidy notes

Record a conversation, lecture or your own thoughts on your phone and get organised notes with key points and next steps.

Meetings & CalendarBeginner10 minVoice notesAI assistantNotes app
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

After an in-person meeting, call or idea walk

Step by step

  1. Record with your phone

    Use your phone's voice recorder app. Ask permission before recording anyone else, and keep the phone close to whoever is speaking.

  2. Get a transcript

    Many phone recorder apps now transcribe automatically. If yours doesn't, upload the audio file to ChatGPT or Gemini, which can work with audio on many plans.

  3. Turn it into notes

    Paste the transcript or attach the recording with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyTurn this recording transcript into clear notes. Context: [e.g. meeting with landlord about repairs]. Give me: 1) A title and date line. 2) Key points as bullets, grouped by topic. 3) Anything agreed, with who agreed it. 4) Next steps with owners and dates. 5) Any numbers, prices or dates mentioned, listed separately so I can check them. Don't add anything that wasn't said. [paste transcript]

  4. Save it

    Paste the notes into your notes app or a shared doc, and add any dates to your calendar.

What you get

Meeting with landlord - 9 Nov. Key points: boiler service booked; damp in back bedroom. Agreed: landlord to send a contractor (landlord). Next steps: contractor visit by 23 Nov; I'll send photos by Tue. Numbers: rent review in March; quote £350 for guttering.

Tips

  • Always get consent before recording others, and delete recordings once you have your notes if they contain personal details.
