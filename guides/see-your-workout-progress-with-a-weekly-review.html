See your workout progress with a weekly review

Log workouts with a quick form, keep them in a sheet and get an automatic weekly progress summary by email.

When to use it

Log after each workout, review weekly

Step by step

  1. Make a workout log form

    Create a Google Forms form with questions for date, workout type, duration, main exercises and weights or distance, effort out of 10 and a note. Link it to a Google Sheet.

  2. Put the form on your home screen

    Open the form link on your phone and add it to your home screen, so logging takes under a minute after each session.

  3. Set up a weekly review

    If your AI assistant offers scheduled tasks or can read your Google Drive (some plans include this), schedule a weekly task with this prompt. Otherwise, set a Sunday reminder and paste the last week's rows yourself.

    Prompt to copyReview my workout log for the last 7 days: [paste rows or point to the sheet]. Compare with the previous 4 weeks. Give me: total sessions and minutes, any lifts or distances that improved, how my effort scores are trending, whether I'm doing too much or too little based on my goal of [goal], and one suggestion for next week. Use a short table and 3 bullet points. Don't give medical advice.

  4. Email it to yourself

    Have the summary sent to your email, or copy it into an email draft to yourself, so you have a record of each week.

What you get

Week 45: 4 sessions, 185 mins (up 20). Squat 5x5 at 52.5kg (+2.5kg). 5k run 31:10 (down from 32:05). Effort average 7.5 - a little high for 4 days. Next week: make Thursday an easy walk or rest day.

Tips

  • Keep the sheet private; even workout data can be personal.
  • If you have pain or a health condition, talk to a GP or qualified trainer before increasing load.
