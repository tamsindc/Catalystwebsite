Run onboarding on autopilot from one form

A new-starter form feeds a tracking sheet, sends you an alert, and kicks off scheduled welcome emails and calendar check-ins for every hire.

HR & HiringAdvanced60 minGoogle FormsGoogle SheetsGmailGoogle Calendar
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every time someone accepts a job offer

Step by step

  1. Create the new-starter form

    Make a Google Form for the manager to fill in: starter's name, role, start date, manager, buddy, equipment needed. Keep personal details like bank info out of it; collect those through your payroll process.

  2. Track it in a sheet

    Link the form to a Google Sheet and turn on email notifications for new responses. Add checklist columns (Contract sent, Equipment ready, Logins set up) with tick boxes, and use conditional formatting to turn a row red if the start date is close and anything is unticked.

  3. Write the welcome emails with AI

    Ask your AI assistant for a set of emails to schedule.

    Prompt to copyWrite 3 short emails for new starters at [business]: 1) sent 1 week before starting: warm welcome, first-day time, address, who to ask for, what to bring, dress code, 2) sent the evening before: a friendly 'see you tomorrow' with the plan for day one, 3) sent at the end of week one from the manager: thanks, what's next, and an invitation to share feedback. Each under 120 words, British English, use [brackets] for details. Then list 4 calendar check-ins to book (with timing and a 3-bullet agenda each) for the first 3 months.

  4. Schedule emails and check-ins

    When a form arrives, fill in the email templates and use Gmail's Schedule send for each one. Create the calendar check-ins with the starter and manager straight away.

  5. Review after each hire

    Ask each new starter what was missing, and update the form, emails and checklist.

What you get

New row: Priya S. | Junior Designer | Starts 7 April | Manager: Leon | Buddy: Kat. Contract sent ✓ Equipment ready ✗ (row red: 5 days to go). Scheduled: welcome email 31 March 9am, see-you-tomorrow 6 April 6pm. Check-ins booked: week 1, week 4, week 8, week 12.

Tips

  • Keep the sheet's sharing limited to people who need it, since it holds staff details.
  • Microsoft Forms, Excel and Outlook can do the same, including scheduled sending in Outlook.
