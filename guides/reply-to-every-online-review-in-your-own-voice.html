Reply to every online review in your own voice

Thoughtful, personal responses to good and bad Google reviews in minutes, so potential customers see you care.

Customer SupportBeginner10 minAI assistantGoogle Business Profile
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Once a week, or when a new review arrives

Step by step

  1. Collect new reviews

    Open your Google Business Profile (search your business name on Google while signed in) and copy any reviews you haven't answered yet.

  2. Ask AI for tailored replies

    Paste the reviews into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyI own [business name], a [type of business] in [town]. Write a public reply to each review below. Reviews: [paste, one per line with star rating]. Rules: under 70 words each; mention one specific thing the reviewer said; thank them by first name only if they used one; for 1 to 3 stars, apologise for their experience without arguing, mention one thing we're doing about it ([what you're changing]), and invite them to contact [email or phone] to put it right; never reveal any private details about the customer; British English; vary the wording so replies don't all sound the same.

  3. Post after a quick check

    Read each reply aloud, make sure nothing confirms private details like what treatment or service someone had, then post it on the review.

What you get

5 stars: Thanks so much, Leah! We're thrilled the cinnamon buns made your Saturday. See you next time. 2 stars: We're sorry the wait was so long on Sunday. We've added a second person on the till at weekends. Please email hello@[cafe].co.uk so we can make it up to you.

Tips

  • Reply to bad reviews within a couple of days; a calm public reply often matters more to readers than the review itself.
  • For health, legal or financial services, never confirm someone was a client in a public reply.
