Turn customer questions into a bank of article ideas

Questions customers email you are gathered in one Gmail label, and once a month they become a ranked list of articles to write.

Content & WritingIntermediate20 minGmailAI assistantGoogle Sheets
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Ongoing, reviewed monthly

Step by step

  1. Create a Questions label

    In Gmail, make a label called Content questions. Whenever a customer asks something interesting, add the label to that email.

  2. Use a filter for common subjects

    If questions often come through your website contact form, set up a Gmail filter (in settings, under Filters) for that form's emails to apply the label automatically.

  3. Turn them into ideas monthly

    Once a month, copy just the questions (no names or email addresses) into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere are questions customers asked [business] this month: [paste questions only]. Group similar questions together. For each group give: how many times it came up, a clear article title, a one-line summary of the answer, and whether it suits a blog post, FAQ entry, short video or email. Rank them by how many customers it would help. Output as a table.

  4. Add them to your plan

    Paste the table into a Google Sheet called Article ideas and pick the top two to write next.

What you get

Asked 6 times: 'Can I pause my subscription?' -> FAQ: How to pause or skip a box. Asked 4 times: 'Is the packaging recyclable?' -> Blog: Where every bit of our packaging goes. Asked 3 times: 'Which coffee for a cafetiere?' -> Short video.

Tips

  • Strip out names, addresses and order numbers before pasting customer emails into any AI tool.
