Check product photos meet listing rules before uploading

A quick pass or fail on every product photo against marketplace rules and your own standards, with what to fix on any that fail.

Video, Image & VoiceBeginner10 minAI assistantPhone camera
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Before uploading product photos

Step by step

  1. Write your checklist

    List the rules for where you sell (for example plain background, product fills most of the frame, no added text) plus your own standards like lighting and angles. Check the marketplace's help pages for its current image rules.

  2. Attach photos and check them

    Attach a batch of photos to your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyCheck each attached product photo against this checklist: [paste checklist]. For each photo, in order, give: Photo number | Pass or Fail | Which rules it fails | What exactly to fix (e.g. 'reshoot with more light from the left', 'crop tighter'). Then a one-line summary of the most common problem across the batch. Be strict but only judge what you can see.

  3. Fix and reshoot

    Reshoot or edit the failures, then run the check again on the new versions before uploading.

What you get

Photo 1 | Pass. Photo 2 | Fail | background not plain, shadow on left | Move to the window and use a white card as a reflector. Photo 3 | Fail | product fills only half the frame | Crop tighter. Most common problem: harsh shadows.

Tips

  • AI can miss small details like a faint watermark or a scratch, so give each photo a final look yourself at full size.
