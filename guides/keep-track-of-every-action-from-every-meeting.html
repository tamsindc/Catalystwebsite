Keep track of every action from every meeting

Meeting notes are saved automatically, and each Friday you get one tracker of open actions across all your meetings.

When to use it

Every Friday afternoon

Step by step

  1. Save every meeting's notes in one place

    Google Meet's 'Take notes for me' saves notes to Google Drive, Zoom AI Companion can save summaries, and Teams recaps sit with the meeting, if your plan includes them. Otherwise, save your own notes into one Drive or OneDrive folder.

  2. Create your action tracker

    Make a Google Sheet or Excel file with columns: date added, meeting, action, owner, due date, status, notes.

  3. Run a Friday round-up

    Open the week's notes (or ask a connected assistant like Gemini to read the Drive folder) and use this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere are this week's meeting notes: [paste or point to the folder]. Here is my current action tracker: [paste open rows]. 1) List every new action as rows: date added, meeting, action, owner, due date (or 'not set'). 2) For existing rows, tell me which seem done or changed based on this week's notes. 3) List overdue actions owned by me first. Output the new rows as a table I can paste straight into my spreadsheet.

  4. Update the sheet and chase

    Paste the new rows in, mark finished items done, and send a short reminder to anyone with overdue actions.

  5. Make it a recurring slot

    Add a 20-minute recurring Friday calendar event called 'Action round-up' with the prompt in the description.

What you get

New: 12 Nov | Marketing sync | Brief designer on poster | Zoe | 19 Nov. Changed: 'Book photographer' looks done (mentioned in Thursday's notes). Overdue (you): Send budget to finance - due 7 Nov.

Tips

  • Ask owners to confirm their actions rather than assuming the AI read the notes correctly.
