Show customers how you performed against the agreement

Turn delivery data into a monthly scorecard for each customer with results against targets, reasons for misses and next steps.

Logistics coordinatorIntermediate30 minAI assistantGoogle SheetsGoogle DocsGmail
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Before each monthly or quarterly customer review

Step by step

  1. Set up a results sheet

    In Google Sheets keep one tab per customer with their agreed targets (on-time, damage, order accuracy, response time) and a row each month with your actual results and notes on any misses.

  2. Ask for the scorecard

    Copy the customer's tab into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyCreate a monthly service scorecard for a logistics customer. Targets and results are below. Please produce: 1) a table of each measure with target, actual, and status (met, close, missed); 2) a three-month trend comment for each measure; 3) a short honest explanation of each miss, using only the notes I've given; 4) 2 or 3 concrete actions for next month with an owner; 5) a 4-sentence summary suitable for the opening of the review meeting. Keep the tone professional and open, not defensive. Data: [paste tab]

  3. Put it in a template

    Paste the result into a Google Docs scorecard template with your logo and check every number against the sheet.

  4. Send ahead of the meeting

    Email the scorecard from Gmail a couple of days before the review, so the meeting can focus on actions rather than reading figures.

What you get

On-time delivery: target 98%, actual 96.4% (missed) - two days lost to the M6 closure. Damage: target under 0.5%, actual 0.2% (met). Actions: add a backup route for Lancashire drops (owner: Sam, by 15th).

Tips

  • Gemini in Google Sheets can help summarise the tab directly if your plan includes it; otherwise copy and paste works fine.
