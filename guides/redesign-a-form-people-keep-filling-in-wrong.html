Redesign a form people keep filling in wrong

A clearer version of a confusing form or report, with only the fields that matter, better wording and a note on what changed.

Business analyst or consultantIntermediate30 minAI assistantGoogle FormsPhone camera
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

When a form or report causes errors, delays or complaints

Step by step

  1. Capture the current form

    Take a photo or screenshot of the form or report, or copy its fields into a document. Blank out any real personal details.

  2. Collect the complaints

    Note the usual mistakes, what the information is used for and who reads it.

  3. Ask for a redesign

    Attach the image and paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyHere is our current [form or report name]. It's used by [who fills it in] and read by [who uses it] to [purpose]. Common problems: [list]. Review it and: 1) list every field and say whether to keep, merge, reword or remove it, with a reason, 2) suggest the best order, 3) rewrite labels and help text in plain English, 4) suggest which fields should be dropdowns or tick boxes to reduce errors, 5) give the redesigned form as a clean list of fields, 6) summarise what changed in 3 bullet points for staff.

  4. Build and trial it

    Rebuild it in Google Forms, Microsoft Forms or your usual template and trial it with a few users for a week.

  5. Agree the final version

    Share the trial feedback and the change summary with the form's owner before replacing the old one.

What you get

Remove: 'Department code' (system adds it). Merge: 'Date requested' and 'Date needed' into 'Needed by'. Reword: 'Justification' to 'Why do you need this? (one line)'. Dropdown: 'Cost centre', to stop typos. Fields cut from 18 to 11.

Tips

  • Ask who reads each field. If nobody can say, it's a good candidate to remove.
