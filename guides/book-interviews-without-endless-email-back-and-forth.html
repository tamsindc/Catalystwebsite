Book interviews without endless email back-and-forth

Candidates pick their own interview slot from your calendar and get a clear invitation with everything they need to know.

HR & HiringIntermediate25 minGoogle CalendarGmailAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Whenever you invite candidates to interview

Step by step

  1. Create a booking page

    In Google Calendar, create an appointment schedule for interview slots, if your plan includes it, or use Calendly's free plan. Add a buffer between slots so you can write notes.

  2. Write the invitation with AI

    Ask your AI assistant to write a friendly, complete invitation.

    Prompt to copyWrite an interview invitation email for [job title] at [business]. Include: congratulations on being shortlisted, the interview format ([video on Google Meet / in person at address]), length ([45] minutes), who they'll meet ([names and roles]), what we'll cover, whether there's a task, a link to book a slot [booking link] by [date], a request to tell us about any adjustments they need, and who to contact with questions. Under 180 words, warm and clear, British English.

  3. Save it as a template

    Turn on Templates in Gmail's settings under Advanced, then save the email as a template for this role.

  4. Send to shortlisted candidates

    Send the invitation individually or with each candidate in BCC, so no one sees anyone else's email address.

What you get

Hi [Name], thank you for applying for Bookkeeper at Hartley & Co. We'd love to invite you to a 45-minute video interview with Sarah (Office Manager) and me. We'll talk through your experience and a short spreadsheet exercise. Please book a time here by Friday 12th: [link]. Let us know if you need any adjustments.

Tips

  • Booking pages show your free slots, so check you're only offering times you can really do.
  • Send a short reminder the day before; it cuts no-shows.
