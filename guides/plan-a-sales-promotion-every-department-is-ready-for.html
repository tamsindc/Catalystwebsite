Plan a sales promotion every department is ready for

One shared promotion plan with dates, owners and a checklist for each department, so stock, staff and marketing are all ready on launch day.

Operations managerIntermediate30 minAI assistantTrelloGoogle Calendar
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Six to eight weeks before a promotion

Step by step

  1. Describe the promotion

    Write down what's on offer, the dates, the products or services involved, the departments that need to act and your budget.

  2. Ask for a plan

    Paste this into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyI'm an operations manager planning a promotion: [describe offer, dates, products]. Departments involved: [e.g. buying, warehouse, store teams, marketing, customer service, finance]. Budget: [amount]. Create a plan working back from launch day with: tasks for each department, a deadline and suggested owner role for each task, dependencies (what must happen before what), and a launch-day checklist. Add a list of the top five things that commonly go wrong with promotions like this and how to prevent each. Format the tasks as a list I can copy into a project board, one task per line.

  3. Put it on a board

    Create a Trello board with a list per department and paste in the tasks as cards, adding owners and due dates.

  4. Book the check-ins

    Add a weekly 15-minute promotion stand-up to Google Calendar with each department lead until launch, and a review a week after it ends.

What you get

Week -6 Buying: confirm extra stock with supplier (owner: buyer). Week -4 Marketing: signage artwork signed off. Week -2 Warehouse: stock received and checked. Common issue: tills not updated with new prices - test on day -2.

Tips

  • After the promotion, paste the board and results into your assistant and ask what to do differently next time.
