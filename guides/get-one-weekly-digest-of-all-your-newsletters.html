Get one weekly digest of all your newsletters

Newsletters skip your inbox all week, then on Sunday you get one short digest of the best bits with links to read more.

Email & InboxIntermediate25 minGmailOutlookAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every Sunday

Step by step

  1. Route newsletters to a Reading label

    Create a Gmail filter (or Outlook rule) for your newsletter senders that applies a 'Reading' label or moves them to a Reading folder, skipping the inbox.

  2. Collect the week's issues

    On Sunday, open the Reading label, select the week's emails and copy their text, or forward them to yourself as one email.

  3. Ask for a digest

    Paste them into your AI assistant with this prompt. If your assistant is connected to your email, ask it to read the Reading label directly.

    Prompt to copyMake me a weekly digest from these newsletters. My interests: [your interests]. For each newsletter give: its name, the 1 to 3 items most relevant to me in one line each, and mark anything time-sensitive (events, sales ending, deadlines) with 'DATE:'. Finish with 'Worth reading in full' listing at most 2 issues and why. Skip adverts and sponsored sections. [paste newsletters]

  4. Save the digest and archive

    Paste the digest into your notes app, add any dates to your calendar, then archive the week's newsletters in one go.

What you get

Garden Notes: plant garlic before mid-Dec; frost-proof pot guide. Town Events: DATE: Christmas market Sat 6 Dec. Money Weekly: energy price cap changes in Jan. Worth reading in full: Garden Notes, for the bulb planting calendar.

Tips

  • Ask it to say 'nothing new this week' for a newsletter rather than padding the digest.
