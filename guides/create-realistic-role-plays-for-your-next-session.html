Create realistic role-plays for your next session

Get a set of role-play scenarios with briefs for each person, a twist to raise the challenge and observer notes for feedback.

When to use it

When preparing practice for any people-skills course

Step by step

  1. Gather a few real situations

    Note three or four situations your learners actually face, such as a customer asking for a refund or a colleague missing deadlines. Leave out real names.

  2. Ask for the scenarios

    Paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

    Prompt to copyCreate [4] role-play scenarios for training [role] on [skill, e.g. handling complaints]. Base them on these real situations: [situations]. For each scenario give: a title; a one-paragraph brief for Person A (the learner); a separate brief for Person B with their mood, what they want and one hidden concern; a twist the trainer can add halfway; and an observer checklist of 5 things to look for. Make them range from easy to hard, and keep the language everyday, not corporate.

  3. Print the briefs separately

    Copy each brief into Word and print Person A and Person B briefs on separate cards, so neither sees the other's information.

  4. Use the observer checklist for feedback

    Hand the checklist to a third person in each group so feedback stays specific and kind.

What you get

Scenario 2: The late delivery. Person A: you are on the help desk. Person B: you have been waiting 9 days; hidden concern - it is a birthday present. Twist: B mentions posting a review. Observer: Did A acknowledge the feeling before the fix?

Tips

  • Ask the AI to play Person B in a text chat so learners can practise on their own before the session.
