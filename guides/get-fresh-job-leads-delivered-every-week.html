When to use it

Every Monday morning

Step by step

  1. Write down your search

    Note your target job titles, location or remote preference, salary floor, sectors to include and any to avoid.

  2. Set up a scheduled search

    If your plan includes scheduled tasks in ChatGPT or scheduled actions in Gemini, create a weekly task using this prompt. If not, save the prompt and run it yourself each Monday in an assistant that can search the web.

    Prompt to copySearch the web for job vacancies posted in the last 7 days matching: titles [list], location [place or remote], salary at least [amount], sectors [list]. Exclude [deal-breakers]. Only include listings from employer websites or well-known job boards. Return up to 10 results as a table: Job title, Employer, Location, Salary if shown, Closing date, Link. Then highlight the 3 best fits for someone with this background: [one-line summary]. If you're not sure a listing is still open, say so.

  3. Log the good ones

    Copy any jobs you want to pursue into your job tracker sheet with the link and closing date.

  4. Check before applying

    Open each link to confirm the role is still open and the details are right, since AI searches can return old or incorrect listings.

What you get

Week of 18 Nov: 7 matches. Top 3: Volunteer Programme Lead, City Food Network, hybrid, £30k, closes 2 Dec. Community Officer, Northgate Council, £28k, closes 29 Nov. Events Coordinator, Hillside Arts, £27k, closing date not shown.

Tips

  • Be wary of listings that ask for payment or bank details early. Genuine employers don't.
  • Tweak the search terms every few weeks based on what's coming back.
