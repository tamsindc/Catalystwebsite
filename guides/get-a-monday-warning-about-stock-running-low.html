Get a Monday warning about stock running low

Each week, get a short email listing items likely to run out, how many days are left and a suggested reorder to check.

Logistics coordinatorAdvanced45 minGoogle SheetsAI assistantGmail
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Every Monday morning

Step by step

  1. Keep a weekly stock sheet

    In Google Sheets list each item with stock on hand, average weekly use over the last 8 weeks, supplier lead time in days and minimum order quantity. Update stock on hand each Friday.

  2. Add a days-left column

    Add a column that divides stock on hand by daily use, and use conditional formatting to turn it red when it's below the lead time plus a safety buffer.

  3. Set up a weekly summary

    Create a scheduled task in ChatGPT (or a recurring Monday calendar reminder if your plan doesn't include it) and use this prompt with the sheet's latest data.

    Prompt to copyHere is this week's stock sheet: [paste or attach]. For every item where days of stock left is less than lead time plus [7] days: list item, days left, lead time, and a suggested order quantity that covers [4] weeks of use, rounded up to the minimum order quantity. Sort by most urgent first. Then list any item whose weekly use jumped by more than [30]% compared with its 8-week average. Keep it to a short table and 3 bullet points.

  4. Send it to yourself and the buyer

    Paste the summary into a Gmail draft to the buyer, check the suggested quantities against open orders, then send.

What you get

Urgent: Pallet wrap 500mm - 6 days left, lead time 10 days, order 48 rolls. Cable ties - 9 days left, lead time 7, order 20 boxes. Use jumped: Bubble mailers A4 (+45%) - check for a large one-off order.

Tips

  • Treat the suggested quantities as a starting point; seasonal peaks and promotions aren't in the average.
