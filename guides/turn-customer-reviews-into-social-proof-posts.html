Turn customer reviews into social proof posts

Your best reviews become eye-catching quote graphics and captions that show new customers why people love you.

Marketing & SocialBeginner15 minAI assistantCanva
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Once a month

Step by step

  1. Collect your best reviews

    Copy five to ten recent reviews from Google, your shop or emails. Use only the reviewer's first name or initials.

  2. Pick the strongest lines

    Paste the reviews into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere are customer reviews for [business]: [paste reviews, first names only]. 1) Pick the 5 most persuasive and pull out a short quote from each (max 20 words, using their exact words, no changes to meaning). 2) For each, note which worry it answers for a new customer (price, quality, trust, speed...). 3) Write a caption for each (under 50 words) that adds context and ends with a gentle call to action. Output as a numbered list: Quote | Name | Worry answered | Caption.

  3. Design quote graphics

    In Canva, search for a 'testimonial' or 'quote' template, add the quote and first name, and duplicate it for the others so they match.

  4. Share them through the month

    Post one a week. Check the reviewer is happy to be featured if the review came from a private email.

What you get

Quote: 'They fixed in an hour what another firm couldn't in a week.' - Priya | Worry: speed | Caption: 'When your laptop dies the day before a deadline, every hour counts. Book a same-day repair through the link in bio.'

Tips

  • Never edit a quote to say something the customer didn't; shortening is fine, rewording isn't.
