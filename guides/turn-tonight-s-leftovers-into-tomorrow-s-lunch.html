Turn tonight's leftovers into tomorrow's lunch

Snap your leftovers and get two or three quick lunch ideas that use them up, with no extra shopping.

Food & HomeBeginner3 minAI assistantPhone camera
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

After dinner, when there's food left over

Step by step

  1. Photograph the leftovers

    Take a photo of what's left in the pans or containers, plus any odds and ends in the fridge you'd like to use.

  2. Ask for lunch ideas

    Attach the photo in your AI assistant and paste this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere's a photo of my leftovers: [describe briefly, e.g. half a roast chicken, some rice, wilting spinach]. Suggest 3 lunches I can make tomorrow in under [10] minutes, ideally ones that travel well in a lunchbox. Only use what's in the photo plus basic store-cupboard items like oil, spices, eggs and bread. For each: name, what to do tonight, what to do tomorrow, and how long it keeps in the fridge.

  3. Prep and pack tonight

    Do the 'tonight' steps straight away and put the food in a labelled container so it's ready to grab.

What you get

1) Chicken & spinach fried rice: tonight shred the chicken; tomorrow fry with rice, spinach and an egg (8 mins). 2) Chicken wrap with yoghurt dressing... Keeps: cooked rice 1 day only, so eat it first.

Tips

  • Food safety matters: follow official food hygiene guidance on how long cooked rice and meat keep, and when in doubt throw it out.
