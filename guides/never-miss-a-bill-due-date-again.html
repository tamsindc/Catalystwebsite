Never miss a bill due date again

Bills arriving by email are automatically labelled, and each one becomes a calendar reminder a few days before it's due.

MoneyIntermediate20 minGmailGoogle CalendarAI assistant
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Whenever a new bill email arrives

Step by step

  1. Make a Bills label and filter

    In Gmail's settings, under Filters, create a filter for emails from your energy, water, phone, council and other billers, and have it apply a label called Bills. Outlook users can do the same with a rule that moves them to a Bills folder.

  2. Check the Bills label weekly

    Once a week, open the Bills label and copy the text of any new bills.

  3. Pull out the key dates

    Paste the bill emails into your AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere are my latest bill emails. For each bill, extract: company, amount due, due date, whether it's paid by direct debit or needs me to pay manually, and any reference I need. Present as a table sorted by due date. Then write one calendar reminder title for each manual bill, in the format: Pay [company] [amount] - due [date]. [paste emails, with account numbers removed]

  4. Add reminders to your calendar

    Create a calendar event two or three days before each manual payment date using the titles the AI gave you, with a notification switched on.

What you get

Bills this month: WaterCo | £38.40 | due 18 Nov | Direct debit Council tax | £142 | due 1 Dec | Direct debit Broadband Plus | £29.99 | due 22 Nov | Manual, ref BP-xxxx Reminder: Pay Broadband Plus £29.99 - due 22 Nov

Tips

  • Remove account numbers before pasting bill emails into an AI assistant.
  • If your plan includes Gemini in Gmail, you can ask it to summarise your Bills label directly instead of copying and pasting.
