Film a month of short videos in one session

Plan, film and schedule eight or more short videos from one afternoon of filming, spread across the month on YouTube Shorts and Reels.

Video, Image & VoiceIntermediate60 minAI assistantPhone cameraYouTubeGoogle Calendar
Open the interactive guideBrowse all guides

When to use it

Once a month

Step by step

  1. Plan the batch

    Ask your AI assistant for a set of scripts that can all be filmed in one place.

    Prompt to copyPlan [8] short vertical videos (20-45 seconds) for [business/channel] about [topics], aimed at [audience]. All must be filmable in [location] in one afternoon with one phone. For each: title, 2-second hook, 3-5 shot script with spoken lines, props needed, and outfit changes if any. Then give a filming order that groups videos by setup to save time, and a posting schedule of 2 a week.

  2. Prepare and film

    Gather the props list, set up near good light, and film in the suggested order. Name each clip with the video number as you go.

  3. Edit in a batch

    Edit all the videos in one sitting in your phone's editor or Canva, using the same caption style and ending.

  4. Schedule them

    Upload to YouTube Studio and set each to Schedule with a date and time. For Instagram, schedule Reels in Meta Business Suite or the Instagram app if your account offers scheduling.

  5. Book next month's session

    Add a recurring monthly event to Google Calendar for your filming afternoon.

What you get

Filming order: kitchen counter (videos 1, 4, 6), garden (2, 7), front door (3, 5, 8). Schedule: Tue and Fri at 6pm through November. Total: 8 Shorts, 3 hours filming, 2 hours editing.

Tips

  • Film a few extra hooks for each video; you can swap them later if one doesn't perform.
