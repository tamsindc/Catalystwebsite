Keep every matter's document index up to date each week

Incoming correspondence is filed by matter automatically, and a weekly session turns it into an up-to-date index the whole team can trust.

ParalegalAdvanced60 minOutlookExcelAI assistantOutlook Calendar

When to use it

Set up once, then every Friday afternoon

Step by step

  1. File emails by matter with rules

    In Outlook's rules settings, create a rule per busy matter that moves emails containing its reference number into a matter folder. Gmail filters with labels work the same way.

  2. Make one index spreadsheet per matter

    In Excel, create columns: Date, Document type, From, To, Short description, Where saved, Action needed. Save it in the matter folder.

  3. Block a weekly indexing slot

    Add a recurring 30-minute Friday event in your calendar called 'Update matter indexes'.

  4. Let the AI draft the new rows

    In the slot, copy the subject lines, dates and senders from the week's matter folder into your firm-approved AI assistant with this prompt.

    Prompt to copyHere is a list of this week's correspondence for matter [reference]. For each item create one row for a document index with columns: Date | Document type (letter, email, court notice, invoice, other) | From | To | Short neutral description (max 12 words) | Action needed (yes/no, and what). Put anything that mentions a deadline or hearing date at the top and mark it DEADLINE. Items: [paste list]

  5. Check, paste and diarise

    Check each row, paste them into the index, and put any deadline into the firm's diary system. Ask the lawyer to confirm any deadline you are unsure about. If the AI mentions any case or law, check it against the original source, as AI can invent case law.

What you get

08/03 | Court notice | [Court] | Firm | Hearing listed for 22 April | DEADLINE: yes, diarise 07/03 | Email | [Other side] | Firm | Asks for disclosure list | Yes: draft by 15/03 06/03 | Invoice | [Expert] | Firm | Report fee | Yes: send to accounts

Tips

  • Never rely on AI to calculate court deadlines. Work them out from the rules and have a lawyer confirm them.
  • Use your firm-approved AI tool only. Subject lines often contain confidential client details.
